Future Nuggets

🔮

Will (The Promise)

Use this to predict the future or make a strong promise. No 'S', no 'ED'. Just WILL + Action.

"I **will** call you later."

Won't (The Wall)

The short way to say "will not". Use it to refuse to do something or make a negative prediction.

"I **won't** do that." / "It **won't** rain."

The 'LL (Fast Track)

Natives rarely say "I will". They smash it together. I'll, You'll, She'll, They'll.

"**I'll** see you tomorrow."

The Instant Choice

Use 'Will' for decisions you make right in the moment. (e.g., The phone rings!)

"**I'll** answer it!"

!

Paisa Secret:

Never say "In the next week" or "The next week". Just say "**Next week**". Drop the 'the' and the 'in'!

Doubt Nuggets

🤔

I think / I hope

Add these before 'will' to soften your prediction. It makes you sound less like a psychic.

"**I think** it will rain." / "**I hope** she'll come."

Maybe / Probably

**Maybe** goes at the beginning. **Probably** goes *after* will.

"**Maybe** I'll go." / "I will **probably** stay home."

Will you...?

The best way to ask for a favor or check someone's future plans.

"**Will you** help me?" / "**Will you** go to the party?"

Will be (The Exception)

The future of "am/is/are". Never say "I will am" or "She will is". Just use **will be**!

"I **will be** late." / "It **will be** fun."

!

The Promises

Good Intentions
  • Money I'll pay you back. (Pagar la deuda)
  • Help I'll give you a hand. (Echar una mano)
  • Contact I'll call you back. (Devolver la llamada)

Street Tip:

If someone asks "Will you help me?", don't just say "Yes". Say "**Yes, I will!**" It sounds way more confident and native.

The Fortune Teller Mission

Record a voice note about tomorrow. Make one prediction and one promise: "Tomorrow, I think it will [Weather]. I promise I'll [Action]!"
