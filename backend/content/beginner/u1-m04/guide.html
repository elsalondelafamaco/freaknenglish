Preposition Nuggets

⏳

IN (The Big One)

Use for "large" time like **Months** and **Seasons**. It's general.

"In December" / "In the summer"

ON (The Specific Day)

Use for **Days of the week** and specific dates. Medium size.

"On Monday" / "On my birthday"

AT (The Exact Point)

Use for the **Clock** and specific moments like "Noon" or "Night".

"At 8:00 AM" / "At noon"

Start vs. End

Don't forget the **"S"** for events. "The concert **starts** at 9."

"What time does it end?"

Paisa Secret:

Think of it like a target: IN is the big circle, ON is the medium, and AT is the bullseye!

Timing Nuggets

⏰

Quarter vs. Half

**Quarter past** = 15 minutes after. **Half past** = 30 minutes after.

"It's half past three (3:30)."

Wait for...

When you stay in a place for a person or event. Always add **FOR**.

"I **wait for** the doctor."

Teen vs. Ty

Hear the difference! **Teen** (long/high) vs. **Ty** (short/flat).

"Fifteen (15) vs. Fifty (50)"

Noon / Midnight

Specific names for 12:00 PM (Noon) and 12:00 AM (Midnight).

"The concert ends at midnight."
