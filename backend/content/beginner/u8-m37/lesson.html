Matching
Vibes

Are you a "Me too" or a "Me neither" kind of person?

When someone shares an opinion, you want to connect. We don't just say "Yes". We mirror their exact energy. Let's learn the fastest way to agree.

The Vibe

Mirror The Energy.

If they give a positive statement, match it positively. If they use a negative word (don't, never, can't), match it negatively!

PRO TIP: Mirror the hidden action word too! If they say "am", you use "am". If they say "didn't", you use "did".

Positive Vibe

"I am ready." ➔ "So am I!"

"I loved it." ➔ "So did I!"

Negative Vibe

"I won't go." ➔ "Neither will I!"

"I haven't eaten." ➔ "Neither have I!"

El pa says: "I don't want to work today."
