Nostalgia Nuggets

⭐

Used to

Use this for **Dead Habits**. Things you did in the past, but **NOT** today.

"I **used to** climb trees every day."

Didn't use to

The negative version. Remember: **DIDN'T** steals the "D" from used!

"I **didn't use to** like broccoli."

Did you use to...?

The question version. **DID** is here, so no "D" on use!

"**Did** you **use to** watch cartoons?"

Was / Were

Use these for your past state or physical appearance.

"I **was** very shy when I was a kid."

Paisa Rule:

If you do it today, don't use 'Used to'. It's only for things that are **DEAD** habits!

Memory Nuggets

🧸

Grow up

To go from a child to an adult. (Past: **Grew up**).

"I **grew up** in a small neighborhood."

Keep

To save an object for a long time. (Past: **Kept**).

"I **kept** my favorite action figure."

Believe in

To think that magic or childhood stories are real.

"I **used to believe in** Santa Claus."

Change

When things or people become different over time.

"I **changed** a lot in ten years."

!

Then vs. Now

Past (Dead Habit)
  • Used to I used to play outside.
  • State I used to be shy.
Present (Reality)
  • Now Now I stay at home.
  • Personality Now I am confident!

Street Tip:

Use **"Did you use to...?"** to start a great conversation about someone's childhood memories.

The Nostalgia Mission

Record a voice note about your childhood. Tell the teacher 3 things:
"I grew up in [City]. I used to play [Game]. I didn't use to like [Food]."
