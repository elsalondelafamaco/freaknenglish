Movie Nuggets

🎬

The Future Movie

Use **Will be + ING** for an action that will be *in progress* at a specific time. You hit 'play' in the future.

"At midnight, I **will be sleeping**."

The Exact Time Trigger

To use this tense, you usually need an exact time marker. Without it, just use regular 'Will'.

"**At 8:00 PM tonight**, I'll be cooking."

This time tomorrow...

The ultimate phrase for dreaming (or complaining) about the next day.

"**This time tomorrow**, I'll be flying to Miami!"

The Ultimate Excuse

It is the most polite and professional way to say "No" to an invitation.

"I can't go to the party. I **will be working**."

!

The Formula

Building the Movie
  • 1. Person (I, You, She)
  • 2. The Engine (will be / 'll be)
  • 3. The Action (working)
Simple vs. Progressive
  • Promise: I will clean.
  • In Progress: I will be cleaning at 5.

Paisa Secret:

Natives always contract this! "I will be" becomes "**I'll be**". It sounds exactly like "Ahl-bee". Try it!

Scenario Nuggets

⏳

What will you be doing...?

The FBI question for the future. Ask this to check someone's availability before inviting them.

"**What will you be doing** tonight at 7?"

Won't be + ING

The negative version. Say this when you are happily visualizing a relaxing future.

"This weekend, I **won't be working** at all!"

Still (Todavía)

Add 'still' after 'will' to show that an action is continuing from now into the future.

"At 10 PM, I will **still** be studying."

I'll be waiting

A classic phrase when someone is running late. It's much smoother than "I'm going to wait".

"Take your time, **I'll be waiting** in the car."

!

The Visions

The Work Grind
  • I'll be having a meeting.
  • I'll be driving to work.
  • I'll be checking emails.
The Vacation Dream
  • I'll be relaxing on the beach.
  • I'll be flying to Paris.
  • I won't be checking my phone!

Street Tip:

When answering "What will you be doing?", don't repeat the whole sentence. Just say: "**I'll be [Action+ing].**"

The Vision Mission

Record a voice note predicting your day tomorrow! Say: "This time tomorrow, I will be [Action+ing]. But at 8 PM, I'll be [Action+ing]!" Paint the movie!
