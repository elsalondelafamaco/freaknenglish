The Rules

The Sketch Artist Rules

You witnessed a bank robbery. You must describe the suspect perfectly. If you mix up 'Is', 'Has', and 'Wearing', the police will arrest the wrong person!

🧍 IS / AM / ARE

Body & Age

He is tall.
I am 30 years old.

👁️ HAS / HAVE

Physical Features

She has blue eyes.
I have brown hair.

👕 WEARING

Clothes & Accessories

He is wearing a hat.
I am wearing shoes.
