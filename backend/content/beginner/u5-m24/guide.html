Pointing Nuggets

👉

THIS (Near / 1)

Use this for **one** thing that is in your hand or right in front of you.

"**This** coffee is delicious!"

THAT (Far / 1)

Use this for **one** thing that is over there, on the shelf, or across the room.

"I want **that** car over there."

THESE (Near / +)

Use this for **many** things that are close to you or that you are holding.

"**These** sneakers fit me well."

THOSE (Far / +)

Use this for **many** things that are far away or in the distance.

"Look at **those** planes in the sky!"

!

The Compass

Singular (1)
  • Near: THIS IS...
  • Far: THAT IS...
Plural (+)
  • Near: THESE ARE...
  • Far: THOSE ARE...

Paisa Secret:

Point with your finger! It makes 'This' and 'That' 100% easier to remember.

The Plural Battle

🆚

The "S" Rule

Most words just need an **S** at the end to become plural.

"One phone -> Two phone**s**"

The "ES" Rule

If a word ends in X, S, CH, or SH, add **ES** to make it plural.

"One box -> Two box**es**"

Irregulars

Some words change completely. No 'S' here!

"One person -> Two **people**"

Over there

Use this expression when pointing at 'That' or 'Those'.

"Who are **those** people **over there**?"

!

Common Gear

In your hand (Near)
  • This key / These keys
  • This card / These cards
  • This book / These books
In the room (Far)
  • That chair / Those chairs
  • That door / Those doors
  • That window / Those windows

Street Tip:

When you are shopping, always ask: "Can I see **that** one?" while pointing.

The Pointing Mission

Walk around your room. Touch 3 things and say: "**This** is my [Object]." Then point at something far away and say: "**That** is a [Object] over there." Bonus points for using **These** and **Those**!
