THE END OF DOGMA

THE EDITORIAL

For centuries, human existence was heavily dictated by religious institutions. Today, however, millions of people are choosing to break away from organized faith. To my mind, this mass exodus is completely logical. Modern society heavily values scientific proof, making it increasingly difficult to believe in ancient, unprovable doctrines.

Furthermore, people are tired of having authority figures preach to them about absolute morality. We have realized that the rigid "rules" of the past often caused far more psychological harm than good. Instead of forcing everyone to take a blind leap of faith, contemporary culture has embraced a much healthier philosophy: live and let live.

Ultimately, being "spiritual" rather than religious allows individuals to seek personal meaning without the heavy burden of institutional guilt. The era of unquestioning dogma is officially over.
