THE BURDEN OF MEMORY

POINT / COUNTERPOINT
THE COMFORTING LIE

Society tells us that a successful life is measured entirely by what we leave behind. Building a corporate empire, accumulating generational wealth, or having a university building named after you is seen as the ultimate human victory.

All things considered, humans desperately want to cheat death. We try to ensure that when we finally pass on, our names will echo through history forever.

THE BRUTAL REALITY

Within three short generations, your own descendants won't even know your name. The obsession with legacy isn't noble—it is pure narcissism and a total refusal to accept our own insignificance in the universe.

When you eventually kick the bucket, your money, your prestigious titles, and your empire stay right here. As the old saying goes: you can't take it with you.
