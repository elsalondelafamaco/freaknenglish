Your Tools

Remember the rules

Before we write and speak, let's remember your grammar tools.

  • 1. Will / Won't ➔ Predictions & Promises
  • 2. Going to ➔ Real Life Plans
  • 3. Will be + ING ➔ Future Action in Progress
  • 4. If + Present, Will ➔ Possibilities
  • 5. Last / Next ➔ Time Markers
  • 6. Did / -ED ➔ The Past
