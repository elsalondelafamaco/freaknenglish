Emotion Nuggets

❤️

The "-ING" Glue

When you use a feeling verb (love, like, hate), the NEXT action MUST have an **-ING** backpack.

"I love cook**ing**." (Not: I love cook)

Enjoy

A level up from "like". It means you really have fun doing it.

"We **enjoy** watch**ing** movies."

I don't mind

The ultimate neutral zone. It means "I don't love it, I don't hate it. It's fine."

"I **don't mind** clean**ing** the house."

Can't stand

Even stronger than hate. It means you absolutely tolerate 0% of it.

"I **can't stand** wait**ing** in line."

!

The Scale

Good Vibes
  • ⭐⭐⭐ Love + ING
  • ⭐⭐ Enjoy + ING
  • ⭐ Like + ING
Neutral & Bad Vibes
  • 😶 Don't mind + ING
  • ❌ Dislike / Hate + ING
  • ☠️ Can't stand + ING

Paisa Secret:

If you say "I love pizza", there's no ING because pizza is a THING, not an ACTION. You only need ING for verbs!

Hobby Nuggets

🎮

Prefer ... to ...

When choosing between two things, connect them with **TO**. Do not say "prefer... than...".

"I **prefer** sleep**ing** **to** work**ing**."

Good at / Bad at

Talk about your skills. Remember, after "at", the action gets an **-ING**.

"I am **good at** play**ing** soccer."

Hang out

The best weekend verb. It just means spending free time with friends.

"I enjoy **hanging out** with my friends."

Sounds good!

The perfect answer when someone invites you to do your favorite hobby.

"Want to grab a pizza?" -> "**Sounds good!**"

!

Free Time

Going Out

Street Tip:

In spoken English, natives drop the hard 'G' at the end. We don't say "CookinG", we say "**Cookin'**". Sound smooth!
