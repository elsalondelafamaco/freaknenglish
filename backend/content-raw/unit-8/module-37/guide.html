Matching Nuggets

🤝

The Lazy Match

If the original sentence is positive, say **Me too**. If it's negative (has a 'NOT'), say **Me neither**.

"I am tired." ➔ "**Me too.**"
"I am not tired." ➔ "**Me neither.**"

The "SO" Mirror (+)

To sound fluent, mirror their helper verb! If they say a POSITIVE sentence, use **SO**.

"I **am** hungry." ➔ "**So am I.**"

The "NEITHER" Mirror (-)

If they say a NEGATIVE sentence, use **NEITHER**. (Don't use 'not' in your answer, neither is already negative!)

"I **don't** like cats." ➔ "**Neither do I.**"

The "DO" Rescue

If the person uses a normal action verb (like, work, play) with NO helper, rescue the sentence with **DO**.

"I love pizza." ➔ "**So do I.**"

!

The Formulas

Positive Agreement (Yo también)
  • So + am + I
  • So + do + I
  • So + can + I
Negative Agreement (Yo tampoco)
  • Neither + am + I
  • Neither + do + I
  • Neither + can + I

Paisa Secret:

If someone says "I hate traffic", you say "Me too" (or "So do I"). Why? Because "hate" is a POSITIVE grammar word! It doesn't have a "not".

Advanced Nuggets

⚡

Breaking the Vibe (Disagreeing)

If you don't agree, don't use 'So' or 'Neither'. Just use the OPPOSITE helper verb and claim it!

"I love sushi." ➔ "**I don't!**"
"I am not tired." ➔ "**I am!**"

The Past Mirror (Did)

If their sentence happened yesterday, your mirror must be in the past.

"I went out last night." ➔ "**So did I.**"
"I didn't sleep." ➔ "**Neither did I.**"

The Future Mirror (Will)

If they are talking about tomorrow, reflect their 'Will' or 'Won't'.

"I will travel soon." ➔ "**So will I.**"
"I won't quit." ➔ "**Neither will I.**"

The Experience Mirror (Have)

If they brag about an experience using Present Perfect, mirror the 'Have'.

"I have been to Paris." ➔ "**So have I.**"

!

The Matrix

You say: "I love it."
  • Agree ➔ So do I. (Me too)
  • Disagree ➔ I don't!
You say: "I didn't go."
  • Agree ➔ Neither did I. (Me neither)
  • Disagree ➔ I did!

Street Tip:

To sound incredibly natural and empathetic, link the sentences: "Wow, **so did I!**" or "Really? **Neither do I.**"

The Twin Mission

Record a voice note finding things in common with a friend. Say: "If you say 'I [Action]', I will say '[So do I / Neither do I]'! We are twins!"
