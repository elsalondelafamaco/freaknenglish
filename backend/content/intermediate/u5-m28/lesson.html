THE TRANSPARENT ERA

INTERACTIVE READING & ANALYSIS

For decades, the idea of constant surveillance felt like a dystopian movie. Admittedly, it is no longer fiction—we are willingly paying for the devices that spy on us. Tech companies don't even need to our accounts anymore; we hand over our personal data for free.

They use complex algorithms to our exact locations, preferences, and habits. You might think you can just of these services, but the reality is that participating in modern society requires a digital footprint. The feeling that is completely justified.

Worse still, this data is sold to the highest bidder. Whether it's corporations on your search history or advertisers practically your online profile across platforms, privacy is effectively dead.

THE MECHANICS

GRAMMAR & PUNCTUATION
Concession Connector

ADMITTEDLY

Used to concede or admit a point that might weaken your argument, usually before stating a stronger counter-point.

"Admittedly, having a smart home is incredibly convenient. However, you are sacrificing all your privacy."

"He is, admittedly, a brilliant programmer, but his ethics are highly questionable."

Punctuation Mark

THE EM DASH (—)

Used to create a sudden, dramatic break in thought, or to add amplified emphasis to a concluding statement.

"It is no longer fiction—we are willingly paying for the devices that spy on us."

"They don't want your money—they want your behavior data."
