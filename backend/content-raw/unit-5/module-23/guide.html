Ability Nuggets

🌟

Can (Super Power)

Use it for things you are able to do. Never use "to" or "s" after it.

"I **can** sing." / "She **can** dance."

Can't (The Wall)

The short way to say "cannot". Use it for the things that are impossible for you.

"I **can't** cook at all."

Play the...

Use "play" for instruments. Don't forget the **"THE"**.

"I can play **the** guitar."

What can you do?

The perfect question to find a talent or a skill.

"So, **what can you do** for the show?"

!

Skill Nuggets

🏆

Well

Use this after the action to show you have skill. Don't say "good".

"I can swim **well**."

Very well

When you are a total pro or an expert at something.

"She can paint **very well**!"

At all

Use this at the end of a **CAN'T** sentence for zero ability.

"I can't dance **at all**."

A little bit

When you are just a beginner. You know some things, but not many.

"I can speak English **a little bit**."

!

The Scale

Degrees (Levels)
  • Pro Level ...very well. ⭐⭐⭐
  • Good Level ...well. ⭐⭐
  • Basic Level ...a little bit. ⭐
  • Zero Level Can't ... at all. ❌

Street Tip:

To ask about a specific skill, just start with: "Can you...?" and keep it fast.
