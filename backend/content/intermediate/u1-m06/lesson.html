THE ECHO CHAMBER

READING & CHECK

"We no longer have debates; we have screaming matches. The internet has created massive echo chambers where we only interact with people who share our exact worldview. The moment someone disagrees, they aren't just wrong—they are evil.

Because of this, people are self-censoring. We hide our true, unpopular opinions because the cost of speaking out is too high. You risk being publicly called out, canceled, or losing friends over a slight disagreement.

But friction is how society moves forward. If we only ever hear our own ideas repeated back to us, we completely lose the ability to think critically. A healthy society requires the bravery to be disliked."

VOCABULARY
From Mod 1

RIP OFF

To overcharge someone or provide a bad deal.

"Paying $10 for a coffee is a complete rip off."

From Mod 2

DOUBLE DOWN

To strongly defend a position, even when criticized.

"Instead of apologizing, he doubled down on his comments."

From Mod 3

LEAD ON

To give someone false hope about a relationship/outcome.

"If you aren't going to hire her, don't lead her on."

From Mod 4

SKIN DEEP

Superficial; only existing on the surface level.

"Their commitment to the environment is only skin deep."

From Mod 5

BITE THE BULLET

To force yourself to do something difficult.

"I guess I'll just bite the bullet and pay the fine."

From Mod 5

HOT MESS

A person or situation that is completely disorganized.

"The project launch was an absolute hot mess."

MARK: I honestly think we need to force people to take debating classes. Right now, everyone just waits for their turn to speak instead of actually listening.

CHLOE: That wouldn't work. People don't care about logic anymore; they only care about winning. Debating is useless because nobody is willing to admit they are wrong.

MARK: I agree that pride is an issue. However, if we implemented a rule where you have to accurately restate your opponent's point before making your own, it would force empathy.

CHLOE: For instance, in politics? Politicians would never agree to that. It ruins their ability to manipulate the crowd.

MARK: Consequently, we keep electing people who yell the loudest instead of the ones with the best ideas. We have to start demanding better arguments.
