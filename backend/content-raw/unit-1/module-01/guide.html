Hello Nuggets

🖐️

Formal Hellos

Use these for bosses, interviews, or people you respect. You can say **Greetings**, **Hi there!**, or base it on the time of day: **Good morning**, **Good afternoon**, or **Good evening**.

"**Good evening**, Mr. Smith."

Informal "Guasá" Hellos

For friends and chill vibes. Start with **Hi** or **Yo!**, then ask: **What's up?**, **What's cooking?**, **How you doin'?**, or **What's good?**

"**Yo! What's cooking?**"

Formal Answers

When someone asks how you are in a formal setting, keep it classy and polite. Don't overshare your problems!

"**Fine, thank you.**" / "**Very well, and you?**"

Informal Answers

Be real with your friends! Say: **I'm good, thanks**, **It's all good**, **I'm alright**, or **I'm doing fine**. If you're having a rough day? Just say: **Been better** or **I'm alive**.

"How you doin'?" ➔ "**Been better, man.**"

!

The Vibe Check

The "How are you?" Flow
  • 1. They ask: How are you doing?
  • 2. You reply: I'm alright.
  • 3. Throw it back: And you?
Time of Day (Formal)
  • 🌅 Morning (Until 12 PM)
  • ☀️ Afternoon (12 PM - 6 PM)
  • 🌙 Evening (After 6 PM)

Paisa Secret:

"What's cooking?" doesn't mean you are in the kitchen! It's exactly like saying "¿Qué se cocina?" or "¿Qué hay de nuevo?". Use it to sound super native!

Goodbye Nuggets

👋

Formal Goodbyes

When leaving the office or ending a formal meeting, keep it professional: **Goodbye**, **Good night**, or **Have a nice day**.

"**Goodbye**, it was nice meeting you."

Informal Goodbyes

Leaving your friends? Keep it short and cool: **Bye**, **See ya!**, **Later!**, **Talk to you later**, or **Catch you later!**

"I gotta go. **Catch you later!**"

Formal Good Wishes

Show respect by wishing someone well as they leave. Use: **Be safe**, **Enjoy the rest of your day**, or the classic **Godspeed** (Good luck/safe journey).

"Drive carefully and **be safe**."

Informal Good Wishes

The best ways to send good vibes to a friend. Use: **Have a good one!**, **Take care**, or **Enjoy yourself**.

"See ya! **Have a good one!**"

!

The Exit Strategy

Good Evening vs. Good Night
  • 🟢 Good Evening = HELLO (After 6 PM)
  • 🔴 Good Night = GOODBYE (Or going to bed)

The Ultimate Phrase:

**"Have a good one!"** is the ultimate American street phrase. You can use it in the morning, afternoon, or night. It perfectly replaces "Have a nice day/night/weekend".

The Icebreaker Mission

Record a voice note using the new vocab! Say: "Hi there! How you doin'? I'm doing fine. Well, talk to you later, have a good one!" Mix the vibes!
