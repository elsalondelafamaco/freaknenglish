Comparison Nuggets

📏

The Short Rule (-ER / -EST)

If the adjective is short (1 syllable), add **-ER** to compare two things. Add **-EST** to say it's the absolute maximum (superlative).

"Cheap ➔ Cheap**er** ➔ The cheap**est**"

The Long Rule (More / Most)

If the adjective is long (2+ syllables), do NOT change the word! Put **More** in front to compare, or **The most** for the maximum.

"Expensive ➔ **More** expensive ➔ **The most** expensive"

The "Than" Bridge

When comparing A to B, you MUST use the word **THAN** to connect them. Do not use 'that'!

"Nike is more expensive **than** Adidas."

The Rebels (Irregulars)

Some words completely break the rules. You just have to memorize them.

"Good ➔ **Better** ➔ **The Best**"
"Bad ➔ **Worse** ➔ **The Worst**"

!

The Scale

Short (Size & Speed)
  • Big ➔ Bigger ➔ The biggest
  • Fast ➔ Faster ➔ The fastest
Long (Vibes & Style)
  • More beautiful ➔ The most beautiful
  • More popular ➔ The most popular

Paisa Secret:

Saying "More better" is like saying "Más mejor" in Spanish. It is completely illegal! Pick ONE: It's either "Better" or "More [Adjective]". Never both!

Retail Nuggets

🛍️

Fit vs. Suit

**Fit** means it's the correct physical size (Talla). **Suit** means it matches your style and looks good on you (Te luce).

"It **fits** me well, but the color doesn't **suit** me."

Too vs. Enough

**Too** means it passed the limit (Negative). **Enough** means it reached the limit you need. Notice the position!

"This is **too** small." / "This is not big **enough**."

Try it on

You don't "test" clothes, you **Try them on**. And because it's a phrasal verb, the pronoun 'it' or 'them' goes in the middle!

"Can I **try it on**?"

Checking the Price

Don't just say "How much?" Point at the item and use the correct verb depending on singular or plural.

"How much **is** this shirt?" / "How much **are** these shoes?"

!

The Mall Map

Customer Survival
  • "I'm just looking, thanks."
  • "Where are the fitting rooms?"
  • "Do you have this in medium?"
Clerks (What they say)
  • "Can I help you find anything?"
  • "It's on sale right now."
  • "Are you paying with cash or card?"

Street Tip:

To ask for a different size without repeating the word "shirt", use the pronoun **ONE**. "Do you have a **bigger one**?" or "This **one** is **the cheapest**."

The Fashion Critic Mission

Look at your closet. Pick two items and record a voice note! Say: "This jacket is [Comparative] than this shirt. But these shoes are the [Superlative]!"
