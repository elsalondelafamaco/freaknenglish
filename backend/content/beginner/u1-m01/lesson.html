LEVEL 3

YOUR TURN

Real Life Practice

Look at the teacher and speak.

Your Turn 1/10

"Say hello to the teacher. Introduce yourself."

You need to use:

Greeting + Your Name

Your Turn 2/10

"The teacher asks: 'Where are you from? What is your nationality?' Answer with your real information."

You need to use:

Country + Nationality

Your Turn 3/10

Ask the teacher to SPELL their name.

You need to use:

The word "spell" in a question.

Your Turn 4/10

"What languages do you know? Tell the teacher."

You need to use:

"I speak..." + Language(s)

Your Turn 5/10

The teacher asks: "What do you do?"

Tell them your job or what you study.

You need to use:

Real life info (Example: I am an engineer, I study medicine).

Your Turn 6/10

You see a famous person. TELL the teacher who it is.

You need to use:

Use "tell" or "say".
(Example: Let me tell you who that is!)

Your Turn 7/10

"Where is the famous person from? Answer with their Country and Nationality."

You need to use:

He/She is from X. He/She is Y.

Your Turn 8/10

How do YOU say hello to new people in real life? Tell the teacher.

You need to use:

Your real answer.

Your Turn 9/10

"You need to leave the class. Say goodbye politely."

You need to use:

A polite goodbye.

Your Turn 10/10

Finish!

Tell the teacher one thing you learned today.

You need to use:

Speak in English.
