Time Machine Nuggets

⏳

The Daily Grind (Present)

Use **Do/Does** for habits and routines. Use **Am/Is/Are + ING** for things happening right now.

"I **work** every day, but today I **am relaxing**."

The Storyteller (Past)

Mix the pasts to tell a great story. Use **Was/Were + ING** to set the scene, and **Simple Past** (ED/V2) for the action.

"I **was sleeping** when the phone **rang**."

The Experience (Present Perfect)

Use **Have/Has + V3** to talk about your life from birth until today. No specific dates!

"I **have been** to Miami twice."

The Fortune Teller (Future)

Use **Going to** for real plans on your calendar. Use **Will** for promises and instant decisions.

"I am **going to** travel. I promise I **will** call you!"

!

The Matrix

The Wall (Helpers)
  • Present: Do / Does
  • Past: Did
  • Future: Will
  • Experience: Have / Has

Street Tip:

If someone asks you a question, listen to the FIRST word. "DO you like...?" -> Yes, I DO. "DID you go...?" -> Yes, I DID. Mirror their helper!

Fluency Nuggets

🗣️

Stop Translating Word for Word

English and Spanish have different formulas. Translate the *idea*, not the exact words. Embrace the English structure!

"I have 20 years." ❌ ➔ "I **am** 20 years old." ✅

The Workaround (Circumlocution)

If you forget a word in English, DON'T panic and switch to Spanish. Describe it! Use "It's a thing that..." or "It's when you..."

"It's the machine you use to clean the carpet." (Vacuum)

Connect the Dots

Stop speaking like a robot with tiny sentences. Use connectors to flow from one idea to the next.

"I was tired, **however**, I went to the gym **because**..."

Buy Time with Fillers

Native speakers don't have perfect answers immediately. They use "filler words" while their brain thinks.

"**Well...** to be honest, **I mean...**"

!

B1 Starter Pack

The Best Connectors
  • Actually, (De hecho...)
  • Besides that, (Además...)
  • Therefore, (Por lo tanto...)
The Mindset
  • Mistakes = Growth. Speak up!
  • Listen to the rhythm, not just the words.
  • Shadowing (repeat after natives).

Street Tip:

Confidence is 80% of fluency. Even if your grammar is messy, if you speak with confidence and good energy, people will understand you perfectly!

The Graduation Mission

Record a final voice note! Tell your teacher: "Actually, I am ready for the next level. I have learned a lot, and I am going to master B1!"
