This Time Tomorrow

Visualizing the future.

What will you be doing?

Imagine tomorrow at exactly 8:00 AM. What action is happening? Today we learn how to talk about actions in progress in the future!

The Vibe

The "Will Be + ING" Rule

When we imagine an action that continues in the future, we use WILL BE and add -ING.

Future Action

Will be + Action(-ING)

👉 "Tomorrow at 10 AM, I will be working."
👉 "She will be inventing a new gadget."

Negative & Questions

Use WON'T BE or start with WILL.

👉 "I won't be sleeping."
👉 "Will you be designing it?"

"El pa had a great idea. 'Mom, tomorrow I will be inventing a new app!'

Gelmy looked at him. 'Oh really? And who will be building it?'

'My friends!' said El pa. 'They will be creating the code, and I will be making the money!'"
