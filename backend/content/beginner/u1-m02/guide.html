Pronoun Nuggets

🧱

The Doers (Subjects)

These are the bosses of the sentence. They perform the action, and they ALWAYS stand **BEFORE** the verb.

"**She** calls Titó." / "**We** want pizza."

The Receivers (Objects)

These are the victims. They receive the action. They MUST stand **AFTER** the verb.

"Titó calls **her**." / "The pizza hit **us**."

The Verb Wall

The action verb is a brick wall. Subjects stay on the left. Objects stay on the right. Do not mix them up!

"**He** loves **her**." (Not: Him loves she ❌)

The Chameleons

"**You**" and "**It**" are masters of disguise. They look exactly the same on both sides of the wall!

"**You** saw **it**, and **it** saw **you**."

!

The Roster

Before the Verb (The Doers)
  • I play...
  • He / She / It plays...
  • We / You / They play...
After the Verb (The Receivers)
  • ...looks at Me
  • ...looks at Him / Her / It
  • ...looks at Us / You / Them

Paisa Secret:

Never say "She loves I". In Spanish we put the receiver at the front ("Ella me ama"), but in English, the 'me' MUST go at the END! "**She loves me!**"

Advanced Nuggets

🧲

The Preposition Magnet

Words like *with, for, to, about*, and *at* are magnets for Object Pronouns. Always use Me/Him/Her/Us/Them after them!

"Come with **me**." (Not: Come with I ❌)

The "You and I" Trap

"Gelmy and me went to the park" is wrong! If you are part of the *Subject* (the doers), it must be "I".

"Gelmy and **I** went to the park."

The "For You and Me" Trap

If the names are *after* a verb or preposition, you become the receiver! Then you must use "Me".

"This pizza is for Gelmy and **me**."

The Secret of "Let's"

The famous word "Let's" is actually a hidden object pronoun! It stands for "Let us".

"**Let's** go!" = "Let **us** go!"

!

The Magnets

Common Combos
  • Talk to him (Not: he)
  • Wait for us (Not: we)
  • Look at them (Not: they)
  • Think about her (Not: she)
Translations
  • With me = Conmigo
  • With you = Contigo

Street Tip:

Native speakers constantly mess up the "You and I" vs "You and me" rule. If you follow the Wall rule perfectly, you'll sound smarter than half of America!

The Posse Mission

Record a voice note about your friends! Say: "My friend is great. **She** always helps **me**. I love hanging out with **her**!"
