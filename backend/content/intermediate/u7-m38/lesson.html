THE DEGREE DELUSION

POINT / COUNTERPOINT
THE COMFORTING LIE

Society constantly tells us that going to college is the only acceptable path to adulthood. The formula is simple: you study hard, get a degree, find a secure corporate job, and live happily ever after. We are told that education is priceless.

Society equates academia with moral virtue and intelligence. For this reason, parents relentlessly pressure their children into taking on massive financial burdens just to secure a piece of paper.

THE BRUTAL REALITY

Modern universities are massive businesses; their primary goal is to sell a product. They convince teenagers to borrow exorbitant amounts of money for degrees that hold almost no real-world market value.

Millions of graduates end up working entry-level jobs that do not even require a university education; for this reason, an entire generation is trapped in a vicious cycle of debt they can never fully escape.
