Advice Nuggets

🩺

Should (Good Idea)

Use this to give friendly advice. It means "I think this is a good idea for you."

"You **should** drink more water."

Shouldn't (Bad Idea)

Use this to warn someone. It means "I think this is a terrible idea."

"You **shouldn't** eat old empanadas."

Must / Have to

This is not friendly advice. This is 100% necessary. The Law!

"You **must** go to the hospital now!"

What's the matter?

The classic question when someone looks sick or sad.

"You look pale. **What's the matter?**"

!

The Symptoms

The "Aches" (Dolores)
  • Headache
  • Stomachache
  • Toothache
  • Backache
Other Problems
  • A fever (Fiebre)
  • A cough (Tos)
  • A sore throat (Dolor de garganta)

Paisa Rule:

We treat sickness like an object. Say "I **have** a headache", never say "I am headache".

Clinic Nuggets

🚑

Take (Medicine)

In English, we never "eat" or "drink" medicine. We **take** it.

"You should **take** a painkiller."

Ache vs. Hurt

**Ache** is a slow, internal pain (Headache). **Hurt** is physical damage (I hurt my leg).

"My head **aches** and my arm **hurts**."

Rest / Stay in bed

The ultimate doctor's advice. Stop moving and sleep!

"You shouldn't work. **Stay in bed**."

I feel...

Use this to describe your internal state to the doctor.

"I **feel** dizzy and tired."

!

The Pharmacy

Meds & Treatments
  • Pills / Painkillers
  • Cough syrup (Jarabe)
  • Band-aid (Curita)
Doctor's Orders
  • Should drink hot tea
  • Should get some sleep
  • Shouldn't go outside

Street Tip:

If you want to sound super natural when giving advice, start with: **"If I were you, I would..."** or **"Why don't you...?"**

The Doctor Mission

Record a voice note playing the doctor! Say: "You have a bad [Symptom]. You should [Good Advice], and you definitely shouldn't [Bad Advice]!"
