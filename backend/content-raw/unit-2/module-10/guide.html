The Tag Nuggets

🪝

The Flip (+ to -)

If the main sentence is Positive, the tag at the end MUST be Negative.

"You **are** Titó, **aren't** you?"

The Flip (- to +)

If the main sentence is Negative, the tag at the end MUST be Positive.

"You **don't** like pizza, **do** you?"

The Pronoun Rule

Never use a name in the tag. Always shrink it to a pronoun (He, She, It, They).

"Gelmy is here, isn't **she**?" (Not: isn't Gelmy)

The Comma

The tag is separated from the sentence by a comma. It's a quick pause before the trap!

"He works here**,** doesn't he?"

!

The Mirrors

To Be (The Boss)
  • Is ➔ Isn't
  • Are ➔ Aren't
  • Was ➔ Wasn't
Normal Actions
  • Play(s) ➔ Don't / Doesn't
  • Played ➔ Didn't

Paisa Secret:

Do not say "You like coffee, no?" or "You like coffee, right?". That's pure Spanglish. Use the Mirror + Pronoun!

Vibe Nuggets

🗣️

Pitch UP ↗️

If your voice goes UP at the end, it means you genuinely don't know the answer. It's a real question!

"You locked the door, didn't you? ↗️"

Pitch DOWN ↘️

If your voice goes DOWN, you are just confirming gossip. You already know the truth.

"You are tired, aren't you? ↘️"

The "I am" Trap

The weirdest rule in English. There is no "amn't I". You MUST use "aren't I" when flipping "I am".

"I am late, **aren't I**?"

Answering the Tag

Don't overthink it. Just answer the reality of the situation with Yes or No.

"You didn't go, did you?" ➔ "No, I didn't."

!

How to Build It

Step-by-Step
  • Step 1: The Vibe Identify if the sentence is (+) or (-).
  • Step 2: The Mirror Find the matching verb (Be, Do, Does, Did) and FLIP it.
  • Step 3: The Pronoun Add He/She/It/They at the very end.

Street Tip:

When you drop your pitch (Pitch DOWN), it sounds super confident. It's the best way to make someone confess!

The Gossip Mission

Record a voice note confirming a rumor about your teacher! Say: "You live in Colombia, don't you? You didn't sleep well, did you?" Pitch DOWN!
