THE
ACCIDENT

Gelmy is furious. Her favorite vase is shattered on the floor, and someone ate her empanada.

Gelmy: "Who broke my vase? And who took my empanada?!"

Titó: "It wasn't me! I was sleeping on the sofa."

El pa: "I didn't do it! I got a cold, so I was looking for medicine in the kitchen."

"Everyone is a suspect. I need to write a report of exactly what happened yesterday at 5:00 PM."
- Detective Gelmy

Let us solve this case!

READING: Gelmy's Notes

INCIDENT REPORT - THE BROKEN VASE

Yesterday at 5:00 PM, someone broke the living room window and the vase. I was cooking dinner in the kitchen when I heard a loud crash.

I ran to the living room. Titó was sleeping on the sofa, but he woke up immediately. El pa came from the kitchen and he looked very nervous.

He said he caught a cold and was looking for medicine. But I noticed my last empanada was gone from the table.

Years ago, El pa used to steal food all the time when I wasn't looking. I think he took it, and maybe he tripped and fell into the vase!
