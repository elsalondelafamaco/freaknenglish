LEVEL 3

YOUR TURN

Look at the teacher and speak!

Your Turn 1/10

"Look in your bag or on your desk. What do you have?"

"I have a/an..."

👉 Talk: How many things are in your bag?

Your Turn 2/10

"What do you NEED right now? Water? Rest? A pen?"

"I need a/an..."

👉 Talk: Are you thirsty or tired?

Your Turn 3/10

What do you WANT for your birthday?

"I want a/an..."

👉 Talk: When is your birthday?

Your Turn 4/10

"Point to an object in the room. What is it?"

"This is a/an..."

👉 Talk: Is the classroom big or small?

Your Turn 5/10

"What do you USE every day for your job or school?"

"I use a/an..."

👉 Talk: Do you work in an office or outside?

Your Turn 6/10

"Look at the teacher's desk. Name ONE thing on it!"

"A/An [object]"

👉 Talk: Does the teacher have a coffee?

Your Turn 7/10

"Hold your phone. Who is the owner?"

"This belongs to me."

👉 Talk: Is it an expensive item?

Your Turn 8/10

"Tell me something you WANT, but you don't NEED."

"I want a... but I don't need it."

👉 Talk: Do you like shopping?

Your Turn 9/10

"Think about your room at home. What is in it?"

"In my room, I have a/an..."

👉 Talk: Is your room messy or clean today?

Your Turn 10/10

WRAP UP!

"Raise your hand and tell the teacher you have something to ask!"

"I have a question!"

👉 Talk: Ask the teacher a question in English.
