Double
Words

Why do native speakers use two words when one is enough?

Welcome to the world of Phrasal Verbs. You add a little word to an action, and boom—the meaning completely changes. Let's break the code.

The Vibe

Action + Direction = New Meaning

Think of it like mixing colors. You take an action (Put) and a direction (On). Together, they create a totally new idea (Wear).

Put ON / Take OFF

Use these for clothes, glasses, or even an attitude. (Wear / Remove)

Give UP

To surrender. To quit trying.

If it's hot inside the house, what should you do with your heavy jacket?

The Story

The Big Date

El pa is getting ready for a date, and the whole family is giving him "advice."

Information Gap Challenge:

Why is Gelmy telling him to "take off" his chain?

El pa: "I don't know what to wear, ma. Maybe I should just give up and stay home."

Titó: "Never give up on love, mijo! Put on a nice shirt and some cologne. The ladies love that."

Gelmy: (raises right eyebrow) "And please, take off that ugly gold chain. You look like a reggaeton singer, not a gentleman."

El pa: "But ma, she likes street style! I hope we get along well tonight..."

Gelmy: "If you look after her and pay for the food, you will get along just fine."
