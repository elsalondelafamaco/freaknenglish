THE HALO EFFECT

READING & CHECK

"I used to be completely invisible. Then, over a summer, I lost weight, changed my wardrobe, and suddenly, the world opened up. People held doors for me. Baristas gave me free coffee. My boss actually started listening to my ideas in meetings.

It was intoxicating, but also deeply depressing. I was the exact same person with the exact same brain. The only difference was my packaging.

Society insists that 'looks don't matter,' but my reality proved that beauty is a currency. We inherently assume attractive people are smarter, kinder, and more capable. It’s called the Halo Effect, and it’s completely unfair."

VOCABULARY UPGRADE

Phrasal Verb

STAND OUT

To be easily noticed or different from others.

"She wore a bright red dress to stand out in the crowd."

Phrasal Verb

BLEND IN

To look like you belong with a particular group.

"Sometimes it's easier to just blend in and not be noticed."

Phrasal Verb

SHOW OFF

To proudly display something to get admiration.

"He bought a sports car just to show off his wealth."

Idiom

SKIN DEEP

Superficial; only on the surface level.

"Physical beauty is only skin deep; character lasts."

Saying

JUDGE A BOOK...

Don't form opinions based only on appearances.

"He looks scary, but don't judge a book by its cover."

Slang

GLOW UP

A major, positive physical or mental transformation.

"Did you see her new style? What a massive glow up."
