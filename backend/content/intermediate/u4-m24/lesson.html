The Post-Truth Machinery

Assuming that the public relies solely on verified journalism is a dangerous misconception in the modern era. Today, misinformation spreads faster than facts. Specifically, algorithmic platforms prioritize engagement over accuracy, rewarding sensationalism.

Historically, corrupt officials had to work hard to conceal their crimes; conversely, today they simply flood the media with contradictory narratives. If voters are overwhelmed with noise, they eventually stop caring. Hence, accountability completely breaks down.

Significantly, studies show that political scandals involving financial bribes rarely lead to severe jail time. Instead of carrying out justice, institutions often allow powerful figures to quietly pay off regulators and walk away unpunished.
