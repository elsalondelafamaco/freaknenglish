👔 The Doer (Subject)

I, You, He, She, We, They.
These are the people doing the action. They ALWAYS start the English sentence.

🎯 The Receiver (Object)

Me, You, Him, Her, Us, Them.
These are the people receiving the action. They go AFTER the verb.
