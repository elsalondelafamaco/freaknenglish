The ATM

The Dream ATM Security

You just found a magical Dream ATM. It allows you to simulate what you would do with unlimited money. But the ATM has strict security grammar rules. If you use 'would' in the wrong place, it freezes your account!

🤔 1. THE CONDITION

IF + Simple Past

Never put "would" or "will" next to IF.
"If I had money..."
"If I won..."

💭 2. THE DREAM

WOULD + Base Verb

This is the imagined result.
"...I would travel."
"...I would buy a car."

✨ 3. THE "WERE" RULE

If I were...

In dreams, "I/He/She/It" always uses "were".
"If I were rich..."
"If she were here..."
