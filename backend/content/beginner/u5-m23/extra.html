The Rules

The Talent Agency Rules

You are a Hollywood Animal Casting Director. You must write contracts explaining what your animals can do. If you break these grammar rules, you lose the movie deal!

✅ 1. NO "TO"

CAN is powerful.
It does NOT need help from 'to'.

I can to jump.

I can jump.

✅ 2. NO "S"

He/She/It do NOT get an 's' with CAN.

He cans run.

He can run.

✅ 3. CAN'T

The negative is CAN'T. Never use don't/doesn't.

I don't can fly.

I can't fly.
