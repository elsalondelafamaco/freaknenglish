The Core Rules

🍕

The Positive Rule (SOME)

When you have a limited but unspecified amount, and the sentence is Positive (+), you MUST use **Some**.

"I have **some** milk." / "There are **some** eggs."

The Negative Rule (ANY)

If there is a 'NOT' in your sentence (don't, isn't, aren't), "Some" is completely illegal. You MUST switch to **Any**. It means zero.

"I don't have **any** milk." / "There aren't **any** eggs."

The Question Rule (ANY)

When asking a general question about existence, we default to **Any**.

"Do you have **any** cheese?" / "Is there **any** water?"

The Polite Exception

We break the Question rule when we are politely *offering* something or *asking for a favor*. Here, we use **Some**!

"Would you like **some** coffee?" / "Can I have **some** water?"

!

The Matrix

SOME (+)
  • ✓ Positive: We need some bread.
  • ✓ Offers: Want some bread?
  • ✓ Favors: Can I get some bread?
ANY (- / ?)
  • ❌ Negative: I don't want any.
  • ❓ Info Qs: Do you have any?
  • ❌ "Don't have some" (ILLEGAL)

Paisa Secret:

In Spanish, we just skip it: "No tengo pan." In English, if you skip it ("I don't have bread"), it sounds unnatural. You MUST add the filler: "I don't have **any** bread."

Fridge Nuggets

🧊

Existence + Some/Any

Combine your new rules with 'There is' (Uncountables) and 'There are' (Countables).

"**There is some** milk, but **there aren't any** eggs."

The "Nothing" Trap

"There aren't **any**" is identical to "There is **nothing**." Do not mix them! "There isn't nothing" is a massive Double Negative crime.

"There isn't **any** food here." = "There is **no** food here."

Out of...

When saying "we don't have any" isn't strong enough. It means you consumed 100% of it and it's completely gone.

"We are completely **out of** coffee!"

Leftovers vs Spoiled

**Leftovers** = Yesterdays food (Use Some!). **Spoiled** = Bad, rotten food (Throw it away!).

"We have **some leftovers**, but this meat is **spoiled**."

!

The Inventory

The Good News (+)
  • We have some snacks.
  • There is some juice left.
  • There are some leftovers.
The Bad News (-)
  • We don't have any beer.
  • There isn't any meat.
  • We are out of everything!

Street Tip:

When you open the fridge and are deeply disappointed, native speakers sigh and say: **"There's absolutely nothing to eat."**

The Explorer Mission

Record a voice note. Open your fridge and tell the teacher: "There is **some** [Food], but there isn't **any** [Food]! We need to go shopping!"
