Grammar Focus: Past Simple vs. Present Perfect

Connect Your Past to Now

How do we talk about life milestones? Some happened at an exact moment in the past. Others started in the past and continue to affect us today. Let's master the distinction.

Structural Rules:

  • Use Past Simple for completed time points (yesterday, in 2021, 2 years ago).
  • Use Present Perfect for life states or durations continuing now (since then, for years, ever).
