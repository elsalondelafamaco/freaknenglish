Ownership Nuggets

🏷️

The Bodyguards (Adjectives)

Words like **My, Your, His, Her**. They MUST be next to the noun to protect it. They can never stand alone!

"This is **my** phone." (Not: This is my ❌)

The Solo Stars (Pronouns)

Words like **Mine, Yours, Hers, Ours**. They already include the noun's meaning, so they stand ALONE, usually at the end.

"That phone is **mine**." / "Is it **yours**?"

His vs. Her (The Gender Trap)

In Spanish, "su" is used for everyone. In English, look at the OWNER. If the owner is a guy = **His**. If a girl = **Her/Hers**.

"It's Titó's car. It is **his** car."

Whose...? (The Interrogation)

The magic word to ask who owns something. Put the noun right after it!

"**Whose** keys are these?" ➔ "They are **mine**."

!

The Claim Chart

Adjectives (+ Noun)
  • This is My car.
  • Is this Your car?
  • It is Her car.
  • That is Our car.
Pronouns (Stand Alone)
  • The car is Mine.
  • Is the car Yours?
  • The car is Hers.
  • That car is Ours.

Paisa Secret:

For guys, the word is the same in both columns! "It is **his** car" AND "The car is **his**". It never changes to "hises"!

Number Nuggets

📱

Phone Numbers (One by One)

We don't group numbers like "treinta y uno" for phones. Say each digit. Also, Zero is usually pronounced as the letter **"Oh"**.

"**My** number is 3-1-0 (Three-One-Oh)."

The "Teen" vs. "Ty" Trap

13 vs 30? For "Teens" (13-19), stress the END of the word. For "Ty's" (20, 30, 40), hit the FIRST syllable hard.

"thir-**TEEN**" (13) vs. "**THIR**-ty" (30)

Age: The "To Be" Rule

Never say "I have 20 years". Age is a state of being, not something you own in a box. Use **am/is/are**!

"**I am** 25 years old." / "**Her** brother **is** 18."

Prices & Money

For money with cents, you can just read the two parts naturally without saying "dollars and cents".

"$4.50 = Four fifty." / "$10.99 = Ten ninety-nine."

!

The Contact Card

Asking for Info
  • What is your phone number?
  • How old is her brother?
  • What is his email address?
Answering
  • My number is 555-0199.
  • He is twenty-two.

Street Tip:

Be very careful! "**Your**" means ownership (Your car). "**You're**" means "You are" (You're crazy). They sound identical, but mean completely different things!

The Data Mission

Record a voice note about yourself and a friend! Say: "**My** number is [Number] and I **am** [Age]. This is **my** friend. **Her/His** number is [Number]."
