THE BODYGUARD
RULE

Why do some words always follow the boss, but stand in front of everyone else?

👉 ASK THE STUDENT: "Who is the boss in your house? Do you ALWAYS follow their rules?"

Some words in English tell us how often something happens.

But they have strict security rules about where they stand in a sentence. We call them the bodyguards.

The Vibe

MEET THE BODYGUARDS

ALWAYS - USUALLY - OFTEN - SOMETIMES - NEVER

RULE 1: They stand BEFORE normal verbs. (They protect the action).

"I always drink coffee."

RULE 2: But the verb "TO BE" is the VIP Boss. They stand AFTER the Boss.

"I am always tired."
