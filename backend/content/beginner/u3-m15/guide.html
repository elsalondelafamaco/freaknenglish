Alibi Nuggets

🕵️‍♂️

The Movie (Was/Were)

Use **Was/Were + ING** for the background action. It's the "movie" playing before the crash.

"I **was cooking** dinner..."

The Photo (Simple Past)

Use the **Simple Past** for the sudden event that stops the movie. A quick "click".

"...when the vase **broke**."

The Glue (WHEN)

Always use **WHEN** to connect the background action and the sudden interruption.

"I was sleeping **when** the phone rang."

"What were you doing?"

The ultimate detective question. Use it to check someone's alibi.

"**What were you doing** at 8:00 PM?"

!

Detective Tools

The "Movie" Rules
  • I / He / She / It -> WAS
  • You / We / They -> WERE
  • Add ING to the action!
Sudden Actions
  • Fell (Caí)
  • Broke (Rompí)
  • Hurt (Lastimé)
  • Cut (Corté)

Reality Check:

Interrupted stories are the best for gossip. Start with the long action and end with the "Ouch!" moment.

Accident Nuggets

🩹

Fall (Fell)

When your body goes to the floor by mistake. Past: **Fell**.

"I **fell** down the stairs."

Hurt (Hurt)

The "Lazy Verb". It is the same in present and past! No 'ed'.

"Yesterday, I **hurt** my back."

Cut (Cut)

Another "Lazy Verb". Use it for kitchen or shaving accidents.

"I **cut** my finger with a knife."

Bleed (Bled)

When the red liquid comes out. Past: **Bled** (one 'e').

"My nose **bled** after the accident."

!

Crash List

Body Parts
  • Leg / Arm
  • Finger / Hand
  • Back / Neck
  • Head / Face
Backgrounds
  • Mop the floor
  • Fix the car
  • Chop onions

Street Tip:

Native speakers always combine these to sound like a movie. "I was fixing my bike when I hurt my hand!"

The Detective Mission

Record your alibi for last night. Tell the teacher: "Yesterday at 8:00 PM, I was [Action] when [Something happened]." Make it sound convincing!
