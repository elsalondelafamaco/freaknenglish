THE LIVE BROADCAST BEATING

VIRAL OUTRAGE

In 1992, violent civil unrest erupted in Los Angeles. During the chaos, a truck driver named Reginald accidentally drove into the epicenter of a riot. He was completely innocent and entirely unaware of the danger. Suddenly, an angry mob surrounded his vehicle.

On live national television, broadcast by a news helicopter, several men pulled Reginald from his truck. They with terrifying, unprovoked violence. One man smashed Reginald's skull with a cinder block. Another danced over his body, celebrating the assault. They completely fractured his head in 91 places and on the asphalt.

The public was traumatized and furious. Millions of citizens watched the gruesome footage. When the attackers were finally identified and arrested, society demanded that they be for life. People wanted the state to make an absolute example of them.

The media demanded blood. The public agreed: there was absolutely no way these men should be for such a vicious, horrific crime.
