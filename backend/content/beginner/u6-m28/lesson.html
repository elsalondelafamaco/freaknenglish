There is / There are

"¡Mijo, there is nothing to eat!"

Today we learn how to take inventory. We are going to talk about what exists, what doesn't exist, and what Gelmy insists is in the fridge even when it's not.

The Vibe

Declaring Existence

THERE IS: For ONE thing (singular) or messy/uncountable things.

"There is a tomato." / "There is some water."

THERE ARE: For MANY things (plural).

"There are three eggs." / "There are some problems."

Negatives & Questions

(-) Negative:
There isn't a car. / There aren't any eggs.

(?) Question:
Is there any milk? / Are there any arepas?

The Story

The Sunday Hunger

El pa opens the fridge looking for a snack. He stares inside for three minutes and yells to the living room:

"Ma, there is nothing to eat!"

Gelmy storms into the kitchen, pushes him aside, and points furiously inside the fridge.

"What do you mean nothing?! There is a tomato, there are some old beans, and there is a lot of attitude in this house!"
