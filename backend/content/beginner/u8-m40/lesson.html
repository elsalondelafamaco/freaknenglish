Real World
Survival

Are you ready to make the tough choices?

Life doesn't care about perfect grammar. When an emergency happens, or when you face a serious moral dilemma, you need to be able to explain your choices and survive the situation.

The Vibe

Make A Move.

When a problem hits, you don't just describe it. You use action words to solve it or reject it.

OFFER / SUGGEST

"I can help."
"Why don't we do this?"

INVITE / ACCEPT

"Come with me."
"Yes, I'd love to."

REFUSE

"No way."
"I can't do that."

If someone says "Let's call a taxi instead of walking", what are they doing?

The Story

The Broken Moto

El pa’s motorcycle breaks down in the middle of a dark street. Titó and Gelmy arrive to "help".

Information Gap Challenge:

Why does Gelmy refuse Titó's idea?

El pa: "Ma, the bike is dead. I suggest we leave it here and take an Uber."

Titó: "Are you crazy? I offer to push it all the way home! Come on, Gelmy, help me push."

Gelmy: (raises right eyebrow) "I absolutely refuse. I am wearing my expensive heels. I am not pushing a piece of metal."

El pa: "I invited my friend to come with a truck. Do we accept his help?"

Gelmy: "Yes. And Titó pays for the gas."
