The Rules

Possessive Adjectives

In Spanish, you say "su carro" for a man, a woman, or a group. In English, you MUST match the owner!

👨 HE

➔ HIS

John has a car.
It is HIS car.

👩 SHE

➔ HER

Mary has a book.
It is HER book.

👥 THEY

➔ THEIR

The parents have a dog.
It is THEIR dog.
