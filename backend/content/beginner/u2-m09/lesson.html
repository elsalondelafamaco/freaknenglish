LOVE IT OR
HATE IT

How do you talk about the things you enjoy doing?

We all have things we love, and things we hate.

But when you talk about an activity you love or hate, English has a very special rule.

The Vibe

THE -ING RULE

LOVE • LIKE • DON'T LIKE • DON't MIND • HATE

RULE 1: Use a Noun (Thing)

"I love pizza."

"She hates traffic."

RULE 2: Use an Action (-ING)

When the thing you love is an action, add -ING.

"I love eating pizza."

"She hates driving in traffic."
