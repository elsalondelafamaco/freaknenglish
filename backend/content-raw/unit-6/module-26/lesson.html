The Vibe

How much vs. How many?

Forget the academic "countable/uncountable" nonsense. Here is the street rule:


MANY: For things you can count one by one.
(Eggs, arepas, tomatoes, problems)


MUCH: For messy, liquid, or collective stuff.
(Water, money, salt, time, rice)

Some vs. Any

SOME (+): I have some coffee.

ANY (- / ?): I don't have any milk. Do you have any sugar?

Exception: If you are offering/asking politely, use SOME. ("Can I have some water?")
