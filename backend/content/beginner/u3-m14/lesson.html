THE MAGIC VERBS

GET is a chameleon! It means: to become (get tired), to arrive (get home), or to obtain (get a job).

But don't mix it up with TAKE (move away), BRING (move here), CATCH (grab transport/sickness), or FETCH (go get and return).

Help El pa explain his condition. Click to reveal the truth:

"I GOT so tired!" (Become tired)
