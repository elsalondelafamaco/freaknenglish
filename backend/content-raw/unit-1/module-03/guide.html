Building Blocks

🧱

The Noun (The VIP)

A noun is a **Person, Place, Thing, or Idea**. It is the absolute star of the show. Without a noun, you have nothing to talk about.

"**Titó** loves the **city**." (Titó = Person, City = Place)

The Verb (The Engine)

The verb brings the noun to life. It is the **Action** or the **State of Being** (like 'am' or 'is'). It makes the sentence move!

"The dog **runs** fast." / "Gelmy **is** happy."

Articles (The Pointers)

Articles always point to a noun. **A / An** means general (any one of them). **The** means specific (THAT exact one).

"I want **a** dog." (Any dog) ➔ "I want **the** dog." (That specific dog!)

The Golden Rule: S + V

Every basic sentence in English MUST have at least one Subject (a Noun) and one Verb. They are best friends.

"**Birds fly**." (Noun + Verb = Complete Sentence!)

!

The Categories

Nouns (What is it?)
  • People: Teacher, Brother, Gelmy
  • Places: Park, Colombia, Kitchen
  • Things: Phone, Pizza, Money
Verbs (What does it do?)
  • Actions: Eat, Sleep, Run, Call
  • State: Am, Is, Are

Paisa Secret:

In Spanish, we can drop the noun if it's obvious ("Corro rápido"). In English, the Verb NEEDS a Noun or Pronoun before it. You must say "**I** run fast"!

Adjective Nuggets

✨

The Adjective (The Decorator)

Adjectives describe the noun. They give it a vibe, a color, a size, or an emotion. Without them, sentences are boring!

"The **crazy** dog runs." (Crazy describes the dog)

The Flip Rule (Crucial!)

In English, Adjectives ALWAYS go **BEFORE** the noun. This is the exact opposite of Spanish!

"I have a **red** car." (Not: I have a car red ❌)

A vs. An (The Sound Rule)

When adding an article before an adjective, use **A** for consonant sounds and **An** for vowel sounds to make it flow smoothly.

"**A** big house" ➔ "**An** ugly house" / "**An** amazing car"

No Plurals for Adjectives

Adjectives are stubborn. They NEVER take an 'S'. Only nouns can be plural in English.

"They are **smart** boys." (Not: smarts boys ❌)

!

The Formula

The Perfect Order
  • 1. Article (A / An / The)
  • 2. Adjective (Beautiful)
  • 3. Noun (House)
  • "A beautiful house"
Adjective Types
  • Colors: Blue, Dark, Light
  • Opinions: Good, Ugly, Smart
  • Sizes: Big, Small, Tall

Street Tip:

If you want to put the adjective *after* the noun instead of before it, you MUST use a "To Be" verb as a bridge: "The car **is** red."

The Decorator Mission

Look around your room. Point at 3 things and use the Perfect Order formula: "This is **a big TV**." Then say: "This is **an amazing room**!" Record your vibe!
