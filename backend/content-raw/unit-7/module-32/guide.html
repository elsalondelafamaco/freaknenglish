Navigation Nuggets

🗺️

Excuse me...

The Golden Rule. Never just yell a question at a stranger. Always start with this!

"**Excuse me**, can you help me?"

How do I get to...?

The ultimate question to ask for directions to a specific place.

"**How do I get to** the hospital?"

Is there a [place] near here?

Use this when you need something (like an ATM), but you don't know the address.

"**Is there a** pharmacy **near here**?"

It's on...

Use the preposition **ON** for street and avenue names.

"The bank is **on** Main Street."

!

Paisa Secret:

Try not to ask "Where is the pharmacy?". It sounds a little rude or demanding. Start with "How do I get to...?" to sound friendly.

Command Nuggets

🚦

Go straight

The most common command. It means "keep walking forward, don't turn."

"**Go straight** for two blocks."

Turn left / Turn right

To change direction. Often used with "at the corner" or "at the traffic light".

"**Turn left** at the intersection."

Walk past

Keep walking until the building is behind you.

"**Walk past** the bank, and you'll see it."

You can't miss it

The classic way to end directions. It means "It's very obvious and easy to see."

"It's the big red building, **you can't miss it**!"

!

Pinpointing

Prepositions of Place
  • Next to Beside something.
  • Across from On the other side of the street.
  • Between In the middle of two things.
  • On the corner of Where two streets meet.

Street Tip:

When giving directions, we don't say "You turn left". Just drop the 'You' and command them: **"Turn left!"** It's faster.

The Guide Mission

Record a voice note telling a friend how to get to your favorite restaurant from the nearest park. Say: "Go straight, turn [Left/Right], and it's [Next to / Across from] the [Place]."
