THE EXPECTATION TRAP

READING & CHECK

"Broadly speaking, we like to pretend that gender roles are ancient history. We talk about equality all day online, but our everyday expectations tell a completely different story.

Men are constantly told to be vulnerable and open with their emotions; however, the moment they show weakness, they are told to 'man up' and deal with it. Women are encouraged to dominate the corporate landscape (which is great), but they are still quietly judged if they don't want to settle down and raise a family.

We haven't actually gotten rid of traditional expectations. We've just added a new layer of pressure on top of the old ones. It's an impossible trap for everyone involved."

DETAIL

/ What contradictory message do men receive about showing vulnerability?

/ According to the text, how does society judge women who choose career over family?

THINKING

/ Do you agree that modern society has just added a "new layer of pressure"?

/ Is it truly possible to completely ignore societal expectations today?
