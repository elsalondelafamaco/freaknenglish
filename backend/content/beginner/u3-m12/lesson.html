El pa just got back from Cartagena. Titó is interrogating him.

Titó: "Mijo! Did you go to the beach? Did you buy a gift for me?"

El pa: "Dad, relax! I went to the beach, but I didn't buy anything. Everything is too expensive!"

👉 Teacher: Ask the student:
"Do you buy gifts for your family when you travel, or do you save your money?"

THE "DID" THIEF

When DID or DIDN'T enters the room, it STEALS the past tense! The action verb stays normal.

But in positive sentences? There is no "Did". So the verb goes crazy (Irregular!).

Help El pa confess. Click to reveal the truth:

"No, I didn't LEAVE early. I SLEPT late!"
