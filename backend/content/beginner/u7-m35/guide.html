The Passive Nuggets

🛠️

The Focus Shift

When the object is more important than the creator, put the object FIRST.

"The song **was sung**." (We don't care who sang it!)

Is / Are made (Present)

Use this for general facts, daily processes, and materials.

"These shoes **are made** in Italy."

Was / Were built (Past)

Use this for history. Things that were created, built, or painted in the past.

"The Eiffel Tower **was built** a long time ago."

Made OF vs. IN

**Of** = The material. **In** = The location/country.

"Made **of** wood." vs. "Made **in** Japan."

!

The Factory

Materials (Made of...)
  • Wood (Madera)
  • Glass (Vidrio)
  • Metal / Gold
  • Plastic / Cotton
The Formula
  • 1. The Object (The car)
  • 2. Is / Are / Was / Were
  • 3. Action (Made/Built/Sold)

Paisa Secret:

We call this the "Passive Voice", but don't panic! It just means starting your sentence with the thing, not the person.

Creation Nuggets

🎨

The "By" Tag

If you *really* want to mention who created it, throw them at the end with **BY**.

"It was directed **by** Spielberg."

Painted / Drawn

Used for art and masterpieces.

"The Mona Lisa was **painted** by Da Vinci."

Written / Directed

Used for books, songs, and movies.

"Harry Potter was **written** by J.K. Rowling."

Discovered / Invented

Used for science, technology, and geography.

"The telephone was **invented** in 1876."

!

The Gallery

Media Verbs
  • Was directed (Movies)
  • Was written (Books/Songs)
  • Was published (Articles)
Creation Verbs
  • Was built (Houses/Bridges)
  • Was designed (Clothes/Apps)
  • Was invented (Machines)

Street Tip:

Don't stress about memorizing 100 irregular verbs. Just memorize the top 5 (made, built, written, painted, directed) and you're good for 90% of conversations!

The Gallery Mission

Look around your room. Pick one object and record a voice note saying: "This [Object] is made of [Material]. I think it was made in [Country]!" Sound like an expert!
