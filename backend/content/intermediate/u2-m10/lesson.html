@CorporateRebel Op-Ed Submission

"I've been a top-performing software engineer for five years. Last month, I finally got a full sleeve tattoo. Frankly, I didn't think it would be a big deal since I just work behind a computer all day!

However, my manager immediately pulled me aside. She told me that while my work is excellent, visible ink is heavily frowned upon by the conservative executives. I was told to wear long sleeves every day, even in the middle of a blazing summer!

Arguably, what I have permanently drawn on my skin has absolutely zero impact on the quality of the code I write. I refused to cover it up, and now my promotion is suddenly 'on hold'."

DETAIL
  • / What did the manager explicitly say about the executives' opinion?
  • / How did the company supposedly react when the writer refused to wear long sleeves?
THINKING
  • / Do you agree that "professionalism" is just a way to force conformity?
  • / If you were the manager, how would you have handled this top performer?

"Dating a coworker is heavily frowned upon in this firm."

COVER UP

To hide something completely from sight.

"She has to cover up her neck tattoo with makeup every day."

Phrasal Verb

STAND BY

To stubbornly defend a decision, action, or person.

"Even though they fired me, I stand by what I said to the boss."

Idiom

"When the pressure was on, the manager showed his true colors."

STREET INTERVIEW

JOURNALIST: "Excuse me! We're asking people on the street about corporate dress codes. Do you think visible tattoos are unprofessional?"

INTERVIEWEE: "Oh, man. Frankly, that whole idea is completely outdated! I mean, look at me. I’m heavily inked, and I manage a whole team of financial analysts. Arguably, my tattoos make me *more* approachable to our younger clients!"

JOURNALIST: "But what if a traditional company asks you to cover up?"

INTERVIEWEE: "I wouldn't work there. I stand by who I am. If a company judges my ink instead of my brain, it shows their true colors. Honestly, to each their own, but I think judging someone’s skin is just ridiculous!"
