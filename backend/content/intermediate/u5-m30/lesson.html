THE FUTURIST'S MANIFESTO

UNIT 5 GRAMMAR CHECK
U.N. ARCHIVES

Admittedly, the rapid advancement of Artificial Intelligence is terrifying. However, we cannot halt progress out of fear. Due to human corruption and greed, our natural ecosystems are failing. Subsequently, we must build a system where algorithms manage our resources efficiently.

In the event that automation takes every job, a Universal Basic Income isn't a luxury: it is a mathematical requirement for survival. Equally important is the regulation of genetic editing. If we don't control it, the wealthy will literally evolve into a separate species.

We are standing at a precipice. We will either engineer a utopia—or program our own extinction.

FACT VS. ASSUMPTION
  • / Does the author assume human corruption is destroying the earth, or is it stated as a fact?
  • / Is the idea that UBI is a "mathematical requirement" an objective truth or a rhetorical strategy?
