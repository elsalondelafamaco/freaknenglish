Today we are going to learn how to be specific. Because if you buy "a" random thing when Gelmy asks for "the" thing... you're in trouble.

The Vibe

Specific vs. Random

A / AN: Just one general thing. Any of them.

"I need a tomato." (I don't care which one)

THE: We BOTH know exactly which one it is.

"Where is the tomato I bought yesterday?" (That specific one)

NO ARTICLE (∅): General plural things or uncountable things.

"I love tomatoes." / "Water is good."

The Sound Rule

A + Consonant Sound:
A banana, A car, A university (sounds like 'you').

AN + Vowel Sound:
An apple, An egg, An hour (the 'h' is silent).
