The "Get" Nuggets

🪄

GET = Become

Use this for emotions or physical states. You weren't X before, but now you are.

"I **got** angry." / "I **got** tired."

GET = Arrive

Use this for locations. (Past: **Got**). For home, don't use 'to'.

"I **got** home." / "I **got** to work."

GET = Obtain

Use this when you receive or buy something. (Past: **Got**).

"I **got** a new job!" / "I **got** a pizza."

Get Lost

When you don't know where you are. Titó's favorite activity!

"I **got lost** in the city yesterday."

!

Get Vibes

Emotions
  • Get happy
  • Get excited
  • Get sick
States
  • Get hungry
  • Get thirsty
  • Get ready

Paisa Secret:

Natives use 'GET' for everything because it's fast. Use it and you'll sound 100% more natural!

Take vs. Bring

**Take** = Move it AWAY (->). **Bring** = Move it HERE (<-).

"**Take** your trash with you!" / "**Bring** me a coffee!"

Catch

Use this for moving vehicles or for getting a virus.

"I **caught** the bus." / "I **caught** a cold."

Fetch

The "Yo-Yo" action. Go to a place, get the object, and come back.

"Can you **fetch** my keys from the car?"

Take a Taxi

Always use **Take** for Ubers, Taxis, or Trains when you travel away.

"We **took** a taxi to the airport."

!

Direction

The Compass
  • Away (->): TAKE
  • Toward (<-): BRING
Transport
  • Moving (Public) -> **Catch**
  • Private -> **Take**

Street Tip:

If you say "I bring my bag to work", it's wrong because work is AWAY from home. Say: "I **take** my bag to work."

The Chaos Mission

Record a voice note about your morning. Use: "I **got** to work at [Time]. I **took** my [Object] with me. I **got** really [Emotion] because of the traffic!"
