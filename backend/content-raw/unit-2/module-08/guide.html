Question Nuggets

🔦

The WH-Crew

The words that start the investigation. **What** (Thing/Action), **Where** (Place), **When** (Time), **Who** (Person).

"**Where** do you live?"

The Do/Does Wall

In English questions, you MUST put a wall between the WH-word and the person. The wall is **DO** or **DOES**.

"What **do** you want?" (Not: What you want?)

Does (The Thief)

For He/She/It, use **DOES**. It acts like a thief and steals the 'S' from the action verb!

"Where **does** he **work**?" (Not works!)

Why vs. Because

**Why** is for the question (Reason). **Because** is for the answer. Never mix them up!

"**Why** do you run? **Because** I am late."

!

The FBI Formula

The Structure
  • 1. WH-Word (Where)
  • 2. DO / DOES
  • 3. Person (You)
  • 4. Action (Work)

Street Tip:

In Spanish, we just change our voice tone to ask a question ("¿Tú trabajas?"). In English, if you don't use DO/DOES, it's NOT a question. Build the wall!

Detail Nuggets

🔍

When vs. What time

**When** is general (Days, months, years). **What time** asks for the exact clock.

"**What time** do you wake up?" -> "At 6:00."

How (The Method)

Use this to ask about the way someone does something, or transport.

"**How** do you go to work?" -> "By bus."

How often (Frequency)

To ask about habits. You expect answers like "Every day" or "Twice a week".

"**How often** do you exercise?"

Who ... with?

If you want to know the people involved, put "Who" at the front and "With" at the back.

"**Who** do you live **with**?"

!

The Suspects

What do they want?
  • Where = Needs a Place
  • When = Needs a Time
  • Who = Needs a Person
  • Why = Needs "Because..."

Street Tip:

To sound fluent, say the first two words fast. "What do you..." sounds like **"Whaddaya..."** in real life!

The Interrogation Mission

Record a voice note interrogating a friend! Ask them: "Where do you live? What time do you wake up? Why do you study English?" Be the agent!
