Superstitions

Facts and possibilities.

If this happens... what will you do?

Every action has a reaction. Today we learn how to talk about things that are 100% true (Science), and things that might happen in the future (Possibilities).

The Vibe

The "IF" Rule

The word IF means 'Si' (condición). We use it to connect two ideas.

100% True (Zero)

If + Present, Present.
For facts and nature.

👉 "If it rains, the plants survive."
👉 "If a plant doesn't get water, it dies."

Possibility (First)

If + Present, WILL.
For future possibilities.

👉 "If you study, you will become smart."
👉 "If you break a mirror, bad things will happen."

Quick Check: If it rains today, I ______ at home.

The Story
"Titó is very superstitious. He tells El pa: 'If you walk under a ladder, a disaster will occur!'

Gelmy rolls her eyes. 'That is not science, Titó. If you water the plants, they survive. That is a fact.'

El pa laughs. 'Well, if I don't study today, what will happen?'"

👉 Ask the student: Do you believe in superstitions?
