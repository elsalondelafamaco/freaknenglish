THE INTERROGATION LOG

UNIT 6 GRAMMAR CHECK
CONFIDENTIAL

While it may be true that the suspect has no prior record, his recent behavior is deeply concerning. He intentionally surrounds himself with a dangerous crowd; nevertheless, he claims he is entirely innocent. To my mind, this is a clear lie.

He knows exactly what is happening in that facility—not to mention, he profits from it directly. We cannot treat this as a simple mistake: it is calculated complicity.

Before long, the evidence will catch up to him. Suddenly, he will realize that nobody can hide their secrets forever...

FACT VS. ASSUMPTION
  • / Does the investigator assume the suspect profits from the facility, or is it stated as a fact?
  • / Is the phrase "calculated complicity" an objective legal term or a subjective accusation?
