Goals, Plans, and Decisions.

Do you have a plan?

Some things are just predictions. But other things are real plans. Today we learn how to talk about your life goals and what you have decided to do.

The Vibe

The "Going To" Rule

If it is a real plan or a decision, we don't use 'Will'. We use the verb 'To Be' + GOING TO.

The Formula

Am / Is / Are + going to + Action

👉 "I am going to plan a trip."
👉 "She is going to decide."
👉 "We are going to choose."

Negative & Questions

Just put 'NOT' after Am/Is/Are.

👉 "He isn't going to choose."
👉 "Are you going to plan it?"

Quick Check: Which one is correct?

The Story
"El pa sat at the table. 'Mom, Dad, I have news. I am going to plan my future. I am going to decide what to study.'

Gelmy smiled. 'Oh really? What are you going to choose?'
