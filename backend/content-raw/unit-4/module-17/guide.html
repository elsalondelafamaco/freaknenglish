Intentions Nuggets

🗓️

Going to (The Real Plan)

Use this for things you *already decided* to do. It's on your calendar!

"I am **going to** travel next week."

Gonna (The Street Sound)

Natives speak fast! "Going to" becomes **Gonna**. Use it when speaking to sound natural.

"I'm **gonna** relax all weekend."

Want to / Wanna

A desire or a dream. You don't have the tickets yet, but your heart wants it.

"I **want to** climb a mountain."

I'd like to...

Short for "I would like to". This is the polite, dreamy way to share a bucket list goal.

"**I'd like to** visit Japan someday."

!

The Formula

Building the Plan
  • 1. Person (I, You, She)
  • 2. Be Verb (am, are, is)
  • 3. The Plan (going to / gonna)
  • 4. The Action (study, sleep)

Paisa Secret:

Never say "I going to". You MUST include the 'am/is/are' boss. "I **am** going to!" Also, don't mix Will and Going to. "I will going" is a crime!

Goal Nuggets

🌍

Are you going to...?

The standard way to interrogate a friend about their weekend or life plans.

"**Are you going to** stay home tonight?"

Learn how to...

A classic bucket list item. Acquiring a new skill takes time, but it's a great goal!

"I want to **learn how to** surf."

Someday

The ultimate dream time marker. You don't know the exact year, but it will happen!

"I'd like to go to space **someday**."

I am not going to...

The negative plan. Use this when you are 100% sure you will avoid something.

"I **am not going to** work on my birthday!"

!

The Bucket List

Adventures
  • Go skydiving
  • Travel the world
  • Explore Europe
Life Milestones
  • Start a business
  • Buy a house
  • Retire early

Street Tip:

When answering "Are you going to...?", just say "**Yes, I am**" or "**No, I'm not**". Don't repeat the whole sentence!

The Dreamer Mission

Record a voice note. Tell the teacher 1 real plan and 1 bucket list dream: "This weekend, I'm **gonna** [Plan]. But someday, **I'd like to** [Epic Goal]!"
