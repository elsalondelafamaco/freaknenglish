Location Nuggets

📍

Next to

Right beside a place. They share a wall or a fence.

"My house is **next to** the bakery."

Across from

On the other side of the street. You have to cross the street to get there.

"The park is **across from** the bank."

Between

In the middle of two specific places. Always name the two places!

"I live **between** the pharmacy and the gym."

On the corner of

Where two streets meet. You usually need to mention both street names.

"It's **on the corner of** 5th Ave and Main St."

!

The Grid

Street Anatomy
  • A block (Una cuadra)
  • An intersection
  • A traffic light (Semáforo)
  • A crosswalk (Cebra)
Preposition Trap
  • **ON** Main Street
  • **AT** 123 Main Street
  • **IN** the neighborhood

Paisa Secret:

Don't translate "al frente de" as "in front of". In English, if it's across the street, say **"Across from"**! "In front of" means it's literally blocking your view.

Delivery Nuggets

🛵

Go straight

The classic command. It means keep moving forward on the same street.

"**Go straight** for three blocks."

Turn left / Turn right

Change your direction. Always specify WHERE they need to turn.

"**Turn left** at the traffic light."

Go past

Keep walking or driving until a specific building is behind you.

"**Go past** the supermarket, it's the next house."

You can't miss it

The perfect way to end your instructions. It means it's super obvious.

"It's the giant yellow house, **you can't miss it**!"

!

The Hotspots

Everyday Places
  • The bakery (Panadería)
  • The pharmacy (Farmacia)
  • The barber shop (Barbería)
Errands
  • The grocery store (Tienda/Mini-mercado)
  • The laundromat (Lavandería)
  • The hardware store (Ferretería)

Street Tip:

To tell someone what side of the street the house is on, use **ON**. "It's **on** your left" or "It's **on** the right side."

The Navigator Mission

Record a voice note helping the delivery guy! Say: "Go straight for two blocks. Turn [Left/Right]. My house is [Preposition] the [Place]." Save the pizza!
