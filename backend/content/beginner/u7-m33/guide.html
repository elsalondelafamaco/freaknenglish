Command Nuggets

🚨

The Boss Verb (Positive)

In an emergency, drop the "You" completely. Start the sentence immediately with the action verb. Be the boss!

"**Run** fast!" / "**Hide** behind the tree!"

The Hard "No" (Negative)

To tell someone NOT to do something, always start with **Don't**. Never just say "No run".

"**Don't** panic!" / "**Don't** look down!"

Let's (Team Survival)

If you are commanding a group and you are included, use **Let's** (Let us). It means "we must do this now".

"**Let's** get out of here!"

Always / Never

For strong survival rules, put **Always** or **Never** right before the verb.

"**Always** carry a flashlight." / "**Never** feed the bears."

!

The Shift

Polite vs. Emergency
  • "Can you sit down?" ➔ Sit down!
  • "Please do not move." ➔ Don't move!
  • "We should leave." ➔ Let's leave!
Survival Vocab
  • Evacuate: Leave the building.
  • Seek shelter: Find a safe place.
  • Stay put: Do not move.

Paisa Secret:

In Spanish, the verb changes completely for commands (corres -> ¡corre!). In English, it's so easy: just say the normal dictionary word loudly! "Run!"

Subjunctive vs Infinitive

🧠

The Subjunctive (THAT)

Triggered by expert phrases (It is crucial that...). The verb drops the 'S' and stays completely naked.

"It is vital **that he stay** calm." (Not: stays ❌)

The Infinitive (TO)

Normal command verbs like *want*, *tell*, or *order* use an Object + **TO** + Verb. They do NOT use "that"!

"I want **him to stay** calm." (Not: I want that he stay ❌)

The Ultimate Mix-up

Never mix the two formulas! "I recommend him to go" is illegal. It's either THAT or TO depending on the verb.

"I recommend **that he go**" OR "I tell **him to go**."

Subjunctive Ghosts (Be/Not)

In the Subjunctive, "is/are" becomes just **be**. Negatives just use **not** (no *don't* or *doesn't*).

"I demand that she **be** quiet and **not** run!"

!

The Formulas

Formula A: Subjunctive
  • I suggest that he run.
  • It is crucial that she be safe.
Formula B: Infinitive
  • I want him to run.
  • I told her to be safe.

Street Tip:

Spanglish speakers always try to say "I want that you go" (Quiero que vayas). In English, "want" NEVER uses "that". It MUST be: "I want **you to** go"!

The First Responder Mission

Record a voice note as a survival expert! Say: "In an emergency, I want **you to** stay calm! It is crucial **that she be** careful, and I suggest **that he not** panic!"
