Deduction Nuggets

🕵️‍♂️

Must be (99% Sure - YES)

Use this when the evidence is so strong, it's the only logical answer.

"Titó has chocolate on his face. He **must be** the thief!"

Can't be (99% Sure - NO)

The opposite of 'Must'. Use this when something is logically impossible.

"He **can't be** the killer. He was with me all night!"

Might / Could be (50% Sure)

When you just have a theory or a guess, but not enough evidence.

"It **might be** a trap. We should be careful."

The "Mustn't" Trap

Never use "mustn't" for deductions! "Mustn't" means "Prohibited" (You mustn't smoke). For deductions, always use **Can't**.

"It **can't** be true." (Not: It mustn't be true ❌)

!

The Certainty Scale

100% Fact (You saw it)
  • It is him.
  • He did it.
Detective Logic (Looking at Clues)
  • 99% YES: Must be
  • 50% MAYBE: Might / Could be
  • 99% NO: Can't be

Paisa Secret:

If you are talking about a crime that happened YESTERDAY, add **HAVE + V3**. "He must **have stolen** it!"

Crime Scene Nuggets

🔎

The Alibi

Your excuse for where you were during the crime. If you don't have one, you're in trouble.

"What is your **alibi** for last night at 8 PM?"

Suspect vs. Witness

A **Suspect** is the person who maybe did it. A **Witness** is the person who saw it happen.

"The **witness** saw the **suspect** run away."

Evidence vs. Clues

**Evidence** proves something (a video, DNA). **Clues** just point you in the right direction (a footprint).

"We found a clue, but we don't have hard **evidence**."

The Motive

The 'Why'. The psychological reason a person commits a crime (Money, Revenge, Love).

"He needed money fast. That was his **motive**."

!

The Notepad

Accusing (The Detective)
  • I suspect that... (Sospecho que...)
  • All the evidence points to...
  • You're lying!
Defending (The Suspect)
  • I swear I am innocent!
  • I was nowhere near the scene!
  • Someone framed me! (¡Me incriminaron!)

Street Tip:

To sound like a true investigator, use **Reported Speech**. "He *claimed that* he was sleeping, but the witness *stated that* he was awake."

The Interrogation Mission

Record a voice note accusing a suspect! Say: "You [must have / can't have] done it! My evidence is [Clue]. What is your alibi?" Close the case!
