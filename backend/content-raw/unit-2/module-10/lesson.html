CONFIRMING
RUMORS

You heard something. Now you want to know if it is true.

In Spanish, we finish sentences with "¿cierto?" or "¿verdad?".

In English, we use a Question Tag. It is a mini-question at the end of your sentence to confirm the gossip.

The Vibe

THE FLIP RULE

To confirm a rumor, you must flip the sentence.

RULE 1: Positive ➔ Negative

"He is rich, isn't he?"

"They live in Paris, don't they?"

RULE 2: Negative ➔ Positive

"You aren't tired, are you?"

"She didn't call, did she?"

Micro-Conversation

CONTROVERSIAL ADULTING

Complete the tags with your teacher. Then, discuss if you agree or disagree with these statements.

Teacher Note:

Have the student complete the mini-question first (e.g. "...isn't it?"). Then ask: "Do you agree?" and let them explain why.

1. Money is the most important thing in life, _______?

👉 Follow-up: Why or why not? What is more important to you right now?

2. Working from home is better than the office, _______?

👉 Ask the student: Do you get distracted easily at home, or are you more productive?

3. People don't really change, _______?

👉 Follow-up: Give me an example. Have you changed your personality in the last 5 years?

4. Life was easier in the past, _______?

👉 Ask the student: What makes life so difficult or stressful today?

5. We spend too much time on our phones, _______?

👉 Follow-up: Be honest, how many hours a day do you spend looking at a screen?

6. English isn't easy to learn, _______?

👉 Ask the student: What is the most frustrating part of learning English for you?
