There has been a terrible crime in the neighborhood. To solve it, you must interrogate the suspects and use the exact right crime vocabulary. One wrong word, and the killer goes free.

Detective Training

Impact & Intent

Before you interrogate the suspects, you must understand the difference between these five crucial words.

HIT

Physical impact (hand, car, object)

FIGHT

A conflict between two or more people

SHOOT

Using a gun or firearm

KILL

Ending a life (can be an accident)

MURDER

Ending a life (planned and illegal)

The Crime Scene

The Death of Don Ricardo

Gelmy just locked all the doors. She is explaining the breaking news to El pa and Titó.

Information Gap Challenge:

Why does Gelmy believe it was a planned murder, and not just a robbery?

Gelmy: "Lock the windows! Don Ricardo, the rich baker, was found dead inside his shop this morning!"

El pa: "What?! Did someone shoot him?"

Gelmy: "No! The police said there was a terrible fight. Someone hit him on the head with his own rolling pin. They didn't just kill him by accident, it was a planned murder!"

Titó: "I bet it was his jealous brother. They had a huge argument yesterday over the money."

Gelmy: "We will see. The Detective is interrogating everyone right now..."
