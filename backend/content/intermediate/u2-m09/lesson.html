THE ILLUSION

CASE STUDY
@StartupSurvivor Posted 1 hour ago

"I used to worship hustle culture. I woke up at 5 AM, ran a side business, and constantly tried to outwork everyone else. I genuinely believed that if I just put in the hours, my efforts would eventually work out.

But yesterday, my company laid off 20% of the staff via a generic email. My manager, who practically lived at the office, was one of them. Seeing that shattered my illusion.

We kill ourselves trying to keep up with an impossible standard; similarly, corporations replace us without a second thought.

I refuse to feel sluggish and miserable just to afford a slightly nicer car. Am I lazy, or am I just finally awake?"

DETAIL
  • / What specific event caused the writer to completely shatter their illusion?
  • / How did the writer use the semicolon (;) and the word "similarly"?
THINKING

  • / Are "side hustles" a sign of ambition, or a sign of a broken economy?

VOCABULARY

HOVER TO REVEAL
Phrasal Verb

WORK OUT

To happen or develop in a successful way; to resolve.

"Don't worry about the presentation, it will all work out."

Phrasal Verb

KEEP UP

To move or progress at the same rate as someone else.

"The workload is so heavy that I'm struggling to keep up."

Adjective

SLUGGISH

Moving or reacting slower than normal due to exhaustion.

"I slept for four hours, so I feel incredibly sluggish today."

Idiom

BURN THE CANDLE

To exhaust yourself by working early and staying up late.

"If you burn the candle at both ends, you will crash."

Slang

HUSTLE

To work aggressively hard, often to make extra money.

"He's always looking for a new side hustle for income."

Slang

QUIET QUITTING

Doing only the strict minimum of your job and nothing more.

"They denied my raise, so I'm officially quiet quitting."
