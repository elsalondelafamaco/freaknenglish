The Vibe

Prepositions of Place

NEXT TO: Right beside it. Neighbors.

"The bakery is next to the bank."

BETWEEN: In the middle of TWO things.

"I am between El pa and Gelmy."

ACROSS FROM: On the other side of the street.

"The park is across from my house."

The Street Rule

❌ Don't translate "In front of" directly!

If you have to CROSS A STREET to get to it, you MUST use "Across from".

Use "In front of" only if there is no street between you (like someone standing directly in front of your face).

The Story

The Missing Dominoes

Titó ordered a new domino set. The delivery guy is lost and calls the house. El pa answers:

"Yeah bro, my house is next to a big dog, and behind a red car."

The delivery guy is completely lost. Gelmy grabs the phone furiously.

"Excuse my son! We are BETWEEN the bakery and the pharmacy, ACROSS FROM the park!"
