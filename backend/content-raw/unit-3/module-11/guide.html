Past Nuggets

⚡

The 'ED' Vibe

To talk about **finished** actions, just add **ED** to the word. Simple!

"I work -> I work**ed**."

Yesterday

The day before today. Always use it to set the past vibe.

"I walk**ed** home **yesterday**."

Last night

Don't say "yesterday night". Say **Last night**! It sounds much more natural.

"We watch**ed** a movie **last night**."

Asked vs. Answered

The back-and-forth of a conversation. Both are regular (+ED).

"I ask**ed** a question, she answer**ed**."

!

Lifestyle Nuggets

🏠

Cooked

Use this for any meal you made yesterday. (Add the ED vibe!)

"I **cooked** a big steak."

Listened vs. Watched

**Listened** is for ears (music). **Watched** is for eyes (Netflix).

"I **listened** to the radio." / "I **watched** the game."

Helped

When you do a favor for your family, friends, or boss.

"I **helped** my mom clean the kitchen."

Finished

The best feeling. Use it for work, books, or projects.

"I finally **finished** my homework!"

!

The Sounds

The 3 Magic Rules
  • RULE 1: THE EXTRA BEAT Only if the word ends in **T** or **D**.

    Wait-**ed** / Want-**ed**

  • RULE 2: THE QUIET SNAP Sounds like a "T". For soft sounds (P, K, S, SH, CH).

    Cook-**t** / Work-**t**

  • RULE 3: THE DEEP VIBE Sounds like a "D". For all other sounds.

    Play-**d** / Clean-**d**

Street Tip:

NEVER pronounce the "E" in "ED" unless you hear a **T** or **D** at the end of the base word!

The Truth Mission

Record a voice note about yesterday. Say 3 true things: "Yesterday, I worked, I cooked dinner, and I finished a book." Focus on those end sounds!
