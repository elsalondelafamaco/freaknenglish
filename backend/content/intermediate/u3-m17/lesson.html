THE DOPAMINE HIT

READING & CHECK

"We live in an era where our self-worth is entirely tied to a screen. People spend hours getting ready just to take a seemingly effortless picture in their bedroom.

Even so, we often feel completely empty the second the likes stop coming in. We know the filters are fake, and we know the smiles are staged. Regardless, we keep participating in this toxic cycle because the fear of being invisible is stronger than our desire for peace.

We are essentially performing our lives for an audience of strangers, hoping they validate our existence. Are we living our lives to experience them, or just to document them?"

DETAIL
  • / What do people spend hours doing, according to the text?
  • / Notice the comma (,) placement directly after the words "Even so" and "Regardless". Why is it there?
THINKING
  • / Why do we keep participating in social media if we know it's fake?
  • / Are we actually "performing" for an audience of strangers?
