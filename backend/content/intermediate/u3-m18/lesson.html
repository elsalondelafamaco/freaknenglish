THE DEBATE COACH

COACH: "Listen up. When defending a taboo topic—whether it's polyamory, extreme parenting, or vanity—never just double down aggressively. If you get angry, you've already lost the argument.

Broadly speaking, people attack what they don't understand. They aren't actually mad at the relationship; they are threatened by it.

If they judge an age gap, in other words, they are projecting their own fears about aging and control.

Your strategy is simple: don't attack back. Acknowledge their discomfort, but focus on boundaries and mutual respect. Force them to explain exactly *why* it bothers them."
