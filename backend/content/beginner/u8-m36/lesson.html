The Vibe

Life Experiences: It’s About THE WHAT, Not The When.

When we talk about life experiences, we don't care exactly when it happened. We just care if you have lived that experience up until today.

"I have eaten sushi."

Did I eat it yesterday? Last year? Who cares! The point is: the experience is in my body right now.

Titó's Wild Claim

Information Gap Challenge:

Based on what you read, who do you think is lying about their past?
