Dream Nuggets

💭

The "If" Dream (Past)

To imagine a fake situation, use **IF + Past Tense**. It's not the past, it's just imagination!

"**If** I **won** the lottery..."

Would (The Result)

Use **WOULD** for the result of your dream. It means 0% reality, 100% fantasy.

"...I **would buy** a mansion."

If I had...

The most common dream. Wishing for something you don't have right now.

"**If I had** a million dollars, I'd travel."

Will vs. Would

**Will** is for real plans. **Would** is for dreams. Don't mix them up!

"I **will** go to work. I **would** go to Paris."

!

The Wishlist

Wild Dreams
  • Win the lottery
  • Quit my job
  • Buy a mansion
  • Travel the world
Smart Dreams
  • Invest the money
  • Start a business
  • Donate to charity

Paisa Secret:

Natives always contract "would". Say "**I'd buy** a car", not "I would buy a car". It sounds 100x more natural!

Advice Nuggets

🎩

If I were you...

The ultimate way to give advice. Always use **were**, never use 'was' here!

"**If I were you**, I wouldn't do that."

I wouldn't...

The negative dream. Things you refuse to do in your imaginary life.

"I **wouldn't** work on Mondays."

Go broke

What happens when you spend all your lottery money and have $0 left.

"If he won, he would **go broke** in a week."

What would you do?

The perfect conversation starter for road trips and parties.

"If you had a million dollars, **what would you do?**"

!

The Formula

The "If" Triggers
  • If I won...
  • If I had...
  • If I were...
  • If I knew...
The "Would" Results
  • ...I'd buy it.
  • ...I'd go there.
  • ...I wouldn't care.

Street Tip:

In rapid speech, "What would you do?" sounds like **"What wudja do?"**. Keep it fast and fluid!
