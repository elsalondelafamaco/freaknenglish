Fusing the Pieces

A phrasal verb isn't just one word. It's a verb fused with a particle (like 'up', 'on', 'off'). Get the combination wrong, and the entire meaning changes.

  • Separable vs Inseparable: Can you put a word in the middle?
  • Pronoun Rule: Pronouns (it, them) must go in the middle. (Turn it off).
