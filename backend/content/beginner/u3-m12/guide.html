Past Trip Nuggets

🌴

The "DID" Thief

In questions, **DID** steals the past. The action verb stays completely normal! No "-ed", no irregular changes.

"**Did** you **go** to the beach?" (Not: Did you went? ❌)

I didn't (Deny)

When you say "No", use **DIDN'T**. Just like in questions, the action stays normal here too!

"I **didn't buy** a gift."

Irregulars (The Rebels)

Forget the "-ed" rule. Irregular verbs completely change their shape in positive sentences. You have to memorize their new forms!

"I **ate** the pizza." (Eat ➔ Ate)

Went & Took

**Went** is the past of GO (for positive stories). **Took** is the past of TAKE (for photos, taxis, or planes).

"I **went** to a fancy hotel and **took** a taxi."

!

Paisa Rule:

If "DID" or "DIDN'T" is in the sentence, the verb goes back to sleep! "**Did** you **went**?" is a massive crime!

The Action Swap

🔄

Bought (Buy)

The past of **BUY**. Use it for souvenirs, gifts, or expensive drinks at the beach.

"I **bought** three shirts at the market."

Came (Come)

The past of **COME**. Use it to talk about when you arrived back home.

"I **came** back on Sunday night."

Left (Leave)

The past of **LEAVE**. When the plane, the bus, or your friend goes away.

"The flight **left** early in the morning."

Slept (Sleep)

The past of **SLEEP**. Crucial for bragging about those late mornings in the hotel!

"I **slept** until noon!"

!

The Swap

Normal ➔ Story (Past)
  • Go ➔ WENT
  • Buy ➔ BOUGHT
  • Eat ➔ ATE
  • Sleep ➔ SLEPT
  • Take ➔ TOOK

Street Tip:

Irregular verbs ONLY change their shape in POSITIVE sentences! The moment you ask a question or say "No", the "Did Thief" resets them to normal!

The Vacation Mission

Record a voice note about your last trip! Say: "I **went** to [Place]. I **bought** [Thing] and I **ate** [Food]. But I **didn't sleep** early!"
