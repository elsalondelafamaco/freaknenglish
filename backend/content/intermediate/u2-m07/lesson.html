CASE STUDY

"I (29M) have been working at a tech startup for two years. The environment is incredibly toxic: 80-hour weeks, zero overtime pay, and a boss who literally screams at us.

I planned to stay for one more year, provided that they gave me the promotion they promised. Instead, they hired someone externally. I was furious.

I originally planned to quit gracefully. Unless you have a backup plan, quitting abruptly is risky. But yesterday, he screamed at me in front of the new hire. I packed my bag and walked out without a word.

Am I the villain for abandoning my team without notice?"

VOCABULARY

Phrasal Verb

BURN OUT

To become completely exhausted from overwork.

"I can't do another 60-hour week; I'm going to burn out."

Phrasal Verb

STEP DOWN

To formally resign from an important position or role.

"The CEO was forced to step down after the scandal."

Phrasal Verb

WALK OUT

To abandon a job abruptly or leave in protest.

"The staff decided to walk out when they canceled bonuses."

Idiom

THROW IN THE TOWEL

To finally give up or admit defeat after a struggle.

"After fighting for a raise, I threw in the towel."

Slang

THE DAILY GRIND

The boring, repetitive, and exhausting routine of everyday work.

"I need a vacation from the daily grind."

Slang

RAGE QUIT

To suddenly abandon a job out of intense anger.

"He was screamed at by a customer, so he just rage quit."

THE BREAKING POINT

TRUE OR FALSE?

Listen to the angry phone call to HR. Decide if the statements are True (T) or False (F).

  • Sarah offers to arrange a team transfer if Mark agrees to stay two more weeks.
  • Mark's primary complaint is that he hasn't received a raise in two years.
  • Mark is willing to stay if the company fires his current boss today.
  • According to company policy, Mark will lose his bonus for quitting immediately.
REACTION
  • / Is Mark justified in giving HR an ultimatum to fire his boss, or is he being unprofessional?
  • / Would you forfeit your yearly bonus to escape a toxic environment immediately, or endure two more weeks?
HR PHONE CALL

MARK: "Hey Sarah, it's Mark from Engineering. I'm calling to let you know I'm officially throwing in the towel. I quit."

SARAH (HR): "Whoa, Mark, slow down. What happened? You can't just walk out. We can look into a team transfer, provided that you give us two weeks' notice to figure things out."

MARK: "Absolutely not. I've been doing 80-hour weeks for a boss who screams at us like we're children. Unless he's fired by the end of the day, I'm not typing another line of code."

SARAH: "Mark, please. Let's schedule a meeting. You know the company policy: immediate resignation forfeits your bonus."
