THE VIBE: GOING TO

INTENTIONS & PLANS

Forget "WILL" for a second. We use GOING TO when the decision is ALREADY made.

  • 📅 It's in your calendar.
  • 🧠 You already thought about it.
  • 🚀 It's a fact waiting to happen.

Coach Tip: You need the verb TO BE (am/is/are) + GOING TO + The Action.
