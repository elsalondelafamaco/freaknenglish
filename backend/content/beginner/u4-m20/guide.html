Time Nuggets

⏳

The "Ago" Machine (Past)

"Ago" means "hace" (time passed). It ALWAYS goes at the very end of the time block, never at the beginning!

"I arrived **two days ago**." (Not: Ago two days ❌)

Currently / Nowadays (Present)

Use these to talk about the modern day or your life right now. They show a contrast with the past.

"In the past I played soccer, but **nowadays** I go to the gym."

The "Last" Rule (Past)

Use "Last" for previous weeks, months, or years. Do NOT put "in the" before it.

"I saw her **last month**." (Not: in the last month ❌)

So far (Connecting Past & Present)

"So far" means "hasta ahora". It connects what happened in the past to right now.

"**So far**, this week has been great."

!

The Anchors

The Basic Jump
  • Past: Yesterday
  • Present: Today
  • Future: Tomorrow
The Daily Jump
  • Past: Last night
  • Present: Right now
  • Future: Later tonight

Paisa Secret:

Never use "Actually" to mean "Actualmente". Actually means "De hecho" or "En realidad". To say "Actualmente", you MUST use "**Currently**" or "**Nowadays**".

Future Nuggets

🚀

The "In" Trick (Future)

To say "dentro de" (how much time from now until the event), use the word **IN**.

"I will see you **in** two hours." / "**In** five years, I will be rich."

The "By" Deadline

Use "By" to talk about a strict deadline. It means "not later than this time".

"I will finish the project **by** Friday."

From... To... (Duration)

Use this combo to talk about how long something lasts across any timeline.

"I lived there **from** 2010 **to** 2015."

Eventually (The Distant Future)

When you know something will happen in the future, but you don't know exactly when. It means "con el tiempo" or "tarde o temprano".

"We will **eventually** move to a new house."

!

The Matrix

The Weeks
  • PAST: Last week
  • PRESENT: This week
  • FUTURE: Next week
The Big Eras
  • PAST: Back then / In the past
  • PRESENT: Nowadays / These days
  • FUTURE: In the future / Someday

Street Tip:

Want to link a story together quickly? Start with "**Back then**, I didn't know much. But **nowadays**, I am an expert. And **soon**, I'll be the boss!"

The Biographer Mission

Record a quick voice note linking your life! Say: "**Two years ago**, I [Past Action]. **Currently**, I am [Present Action]. And **in one year**, I will [Future Action]!"
