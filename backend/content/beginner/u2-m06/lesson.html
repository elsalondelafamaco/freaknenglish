The Vibe

The "Do & Does" Rule

In English, we change the action depending on WHO is doing it.

I / You / We / They

The verb is normal.

👉 "I work hard."
👉 "They study English."

Negative: Add DON'T.

He / She / It (The VIPs)

Add an -S to the verb!

👉 "Gelmy learnS fast."
👉 "Titó workS every day."

Negative: Add DOESN'T (and NO -S).

Quick Check: "El pa" (He)?

The Story
"My parents are very strict. My mom, Gelmy, teaches me English every day. My dad, Titó, doesn't speak it well, but he tries to help.

Me? I am El pa. I learn when I want to. I don't stress about the future."

👉 Ask the student: Are you relaxed like El pa, or strict like Gelmy?

Find the action: What does Gelmy do every day?
