THE INTERNET MOB

READING & CHECK

Cancel culture has completely toxicized the internet. People love to pretend they are righteous activists; for instance, if a celebrity makes an offensive joke from ten years ago, the internet mob demands their career be destroyed immediately. We no longer allow room for growth.

They call it "accountability," but it looks a lot like revenge. For instance, look at normal, everyday people who get fired from their jobs over a misunderstood 10-second video recorded by a stranger.

We are living in a digital witch hunt where the punishment rarely fits the crime. The internet doesn't want apologies; it wants "blood."

VOCABULARY UPGRADE

Phrasal Verb

CALL OUT

To publicly criticize someone for their bad behavior.

"She was called out for lying on Twitter."

Phrasal Verb

BACK DOWN

To withdraw a claim or surrender in an argument.

"Despite the massive backlash, he refused to back down."

Phrasal Verb

DOUBLE DOWN

To strengthen one's commitment to a risky stance.

"Instead of apologizing, she doubled down on her controversial opinion."

Idiom

JUMP ON THE BANDWAGON

To join a popular trend just because others are doing it.

"Once the hashtag went viral, everyone jumped on the bandwagon."

Slang

WOKE

Aware of social injustices (often used mockingly today).

"He tries way too hard to sound woke in all his posts."

Slang

TROLL

Someone who posts inflammatory comments to provoke people.

"Don't even reply to him, he's just an internet troll."
