THE "DEAD HABIT"

When we talk about habits or realities that died in the past, we use USED TO. It means "In the past YES, but today NO."

Remember our friend DIDN'T? If he is in the sentence, he steals the "D" -> Didn't use to.

Help El pa admit his past. Click to reveal the truth:

"I USED TO CRY when I didn't get candy!"
