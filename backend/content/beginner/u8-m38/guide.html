The Golden Nuggets 🍔

The "IT" Sandwich

If you replace the object with a pronoun (IT or THEM), it MUST go in the middle. It becomes the meat of the sandwich.

Example: "Turn IT off." (Not: Turn off it)

Turn on / Turn off

Use these for anything with electricity, batteries, or a switch (TV, lights, phone).

Example: "Turn the lights off before you sleep."

Put on / Take off

Use these ONLY for clothing, shoes, and accessories. Do NOT say "use a jacket".

Example: "It's cold. Put on your jacket."

! The Menu

With the Noun (2 Options)

  • ✓ Turn off the TV.
  • ✓ Turn the TV off.

With Pronouns (IT / THEM)

  • ✓ Turn it off.
  • ❌ Turn off it. (ILLEGAL!)
  • ✓ Put them on.

Paisa Secret:

When natives speak fast, the pronoun blends in. "Turn it off" sounds like "Turn-i-doff". "Put it on" sounds like "Puh-di-don".

Chore Nuggets 🧹

Pick up / Put down

Pick up = Lift from the floor/table.
Put down = Return it to the surface.

Example: "Pick up your shoes and put them down in the closet."

Throw away

To put something in the trash. It is 100% garbage now.

Example: "This pizza is old. Throw it away."

Put away

Different from throwing away! This means putting things back in their proper storage place.

Example: "Put away the clean dishes."

Look for (Unbreakable)

This verb is Unbreakable. You can NEVER separate it. The "it" goes at the very end.

Example: "I lost my keys. I am looking for them."

! Chore Chart

Cleaning Up

  • Take out the trash.
  • Clean up the kitchen.
  • Wash up after eating.

Paperwork

  • Fill out the form. (Llenar)
  • Write it down. (Anotar)

Street Tip:

To sound like an angry roommate, combine them quickly:
"Pick it up, clean it up, and take it out!"

🚀 The Roommate Mission

Record a voice note giving orders to a lazy roommate. Use the pronouns! Say:
"Your shirt is on the floor. Pick it up! The TV is loud. Turn it off!"
