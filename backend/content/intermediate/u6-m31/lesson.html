THE DECRIMINALIZATION DEBATE

THE ARTICLE

For centuries, polite society has chosen to look down on sex work, treating it as a moral failure rather than an economic transaction. However, advocates argue for complete decriminalization—a move that prioritizes bodily autonomy and worker safety.

When authorities try to shut down the industry through criminalization, they simply force workers into dangerous, unregulated environments; not to mention, they empower abusive syndicates.

Instead of forcing the government to turn a blind eye to reality, we should listen when sex workers speak out about their rights. A modern hustler deserves the exact same legal protections—and basic human dignity—as any other independent contractor.
