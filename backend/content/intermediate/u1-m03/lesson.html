THE SWIPE FATIGUE

READING & CHECK

"I deleted all my dating apps yesterday. It’s absolutely exhausting. You match with someone, exchange three generic messages about how your weekend went, and then they disappear. It’s a never-ending cycle of false hope.

We are starting to treat human beings like products in a catalog. If someone has a slightly weird laugh or texts back too slowly, we just swipe left and look for the 'perfect' upgrade. The paradox of choice is destroying us.

Nobody wants to put in the effort to actually get to know a flawed, real person anymore. We want instant chemistry without the work. I'm done shopping for humans."

VOCABULARY UPGRADE

Phrasal Verb

HIT IT OFF

To instantly have a great connection.

"We met for coffee and immediately hit it off."

Phrasal Verb

LEAD ON

To give false hope about a relationship.

"She wasn't interested, but she kept leading him on."

Phrasal Verb

SETTLE DOWN

To commit to a stable relationship.

"He's 35 and finally ready to settle down."

Idiom

PLENTY OF FISH

There are many other potential partners.

"Don't cry over him, there are plenty of fish."

Slang

GHOSTING

Cutting off contact without explanation.

"Everything was going well until the ghosting."
