Article Nuggets

👉

A / An (General)

Use these for **one** thing when you don't care *which* one. It's just one of many. (Use **An** if the next word starts with a vowel sound).

"I want **an** apple." (Any apple in the world is fine).

The (Specific)

Use "The" when both you and the listener know **exactly which one** you are talking about.

"I want **the** apple on the table." (That exact one).

The "Storyteller" Rule

The first time you mention something, use **A/An**. The second time you mention it, it becomes specific, so use **The**.

"I saw **a** dog. **The** dog was cute."

No Article (Zero Article)

When talking about things **in general** (plural nouns) or abstract concepts (love, time, music), use NO article.

"I love **dogs** and **music**." (Not: I love the dogs ❌)

!

The Pointers

When to use "THE"
  • ✓ Unique things: The sun, The moon
  • ✓ Specific places: The bank, The park
  • ✓ Superlatives: The best, The biggest
When to use NOTHING (Ø)
  • ❌ General plurals: Cars are fast.
  • ❌ Continents/Countries: Colombia, Asia
  • ❌ Meals/Sports: Lunch, Soccer

Paisa Secret:

In Spanish we say "El amor es complicado." In English, abstract nouns get ZERO article. You must say "**Love** is complicated" (Never "The love").

Mystery Nuggets

❓

Some- (Positive Mysteries)

Use **Something, Someone, Somewhere** for positive sentences (+) when you don't know the exact thing/person/place.

"**Someone** is at the door!"

Any- (Questions & Negatives)

If the sentence has a 'not' (-) or is a question (?), switch to **Anything, Anyone, Anywhere**.

"I didn't see **anyone**. Is **anything** wrong?"

Every- (The 100% Rule)

Use **Everything, Everyone, Everywhere** to mean ALL of them. Grammar Trap: Treat them as SINGULAR!

"**Everyone is** here." (Not: Everyone are ❌)

No- (The Double Negative Trap)

**Nothing, No one, Nowhere** already carry a "No". Because English hates double negatives, the verb MUST be positive!

"I have **nothing**." (Not: I don't have nothing ❌)

!

The Matrix

By Category
  • Things (-thing): Something, Anything
  • People (-one/-body): Someone, Anybody
  • Places (-where): Somewhere, Anywhere
The "No Sé Nada" Formula
  • ✓ Option A: "I don't know anything."
  • ✓ Option B: "I know nothing."

Street Tip:

There is ONE time you can use "Some-" in a question: when you are offering or asking for something politely! "Would you like **something** to drink?"

The Detective Mission

Record a voice note about a mystery! Say: "**Someone** is in the house, but I don't see **anyone**. There is **nothing** in the living room. **Everyone** is scared!"
