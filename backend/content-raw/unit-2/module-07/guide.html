Placement Nuggets

🛡️

The Bodyguard (Normal Verbs)

The frequency word is a bodyguard. It stands **BEFORE** normal verbs to protect them.

"I **always** wake up early."

The Boss (To Be)

The verb TO BE (am/is/are) is the boss. The bodyguard must stand **AFTER** the boss.

"I am **always** late."

Never (The Terminator)

"Never" is already negative. Don't use "don't" or "isn't" with it. Double negatives are a crime!

"She **never** eats meat." (Not: She doesn't never...)

Sometimes (The Rebel)

This word can follow the rules, or it can jump to the very front of the sentence.

"**Sometimes** I go to the gym."

!

The Meter

High Frequency
  • 100% Always
  • 80% Usually
  • 70% Often
Low Frequency
  • 50% Sometimes
  • 10% Hardly ever
  • 0% Never

Paisa Secret:

In Spanish we say "Yo siempre estoy", but in English you MUST say "I am always". Respect the boss!

Math Nuggets

📅

How often...?

The ultimate question to ask about someone's routine or habits.

"**How often** do you go to the gym?"

Once / Twice

"One time" is okay, but **Once** is better. Same for "Two times" -> **Twice**.

"I play soccer **twice** a week."

Every...

Means "todos los". Use it with any time word (day, week, month).

"I study English **every day**."

The End of the Line

Multi-word phrases (like "once a week" or "every day") ALWAYS go at the end of the sentence. Never in the middle!

"I visit my mom **once a month**."

!

The Formula

Step 1: The Number
  • 1x = Once
  • 2x = Twice
  • 3x = Three times
  • 4x = Four times
Step 2: The Timeframe
  • ... a day
  • ... a week
  • ... a month

Street Tip:

Native speakers talk fast. "Once a week" often sounds like **"Wun-sa-week"**. Listen for the connection!

The Habit Mission

Record a voice note about your week. Say: "I **always** [Action]. I **rarely** [Action]. And I [Action] **twice a week**!"
