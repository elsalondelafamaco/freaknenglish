THE MECHANICS

GRAMMAR & PUNCTUATION
Cause & Effect

DUE TO / AS A RESULT

Used to explicitly show the direct consequence of a preceding action, policy, or situation.

"The oceans are rapidly warming. As a result, coral reefs are dying."

"Due to corporate negligence, the river was completely polluted."

Punctuation Mark

THE COLON (:)

Used to introduce an explanation, a list, or a dramatic conclusion that directly answers the previous clause.

"They only care about one thing: their quarterly profits."

"Scientists agree on one terrifying fact: time is running out."
