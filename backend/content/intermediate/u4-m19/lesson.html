THE ULTIMATE PRICE

READING & CHECK

"The debate around capital punishment often centers on morality, but we must also look at the practical implications. Proponents argue that the death penalty deters violent crime, providing the ultimate warning to potential offenders. They believe that executing a murderer brings closure to the victims' families.

However, studies consistently show no correlation between the death penalty and lower crime rates. Furthermore, the justice system is not infallible; innocent people have been sentenced to death and exonerated only decades later.

When the state holds the power of life and death, the risk of executing an innocent person is a price too high for civilized society to pay."

FACT VS. ASSUMPTION
  • / Does the text present the idea that execution brings "closure" as a proven fact or an assumption?

VOCABULARY UPGRADE

Phrasal Verb

LOCK UP

To imprison someone in a jail or cell.

"They should lock him up and throw away the key."

Phrasal Verb

GET AWAY WITH

To escape punishment for a bad act.

"He thought he could get away with the robbery."

Phrasal Verb

CARRY OUT

To perform a task or execute a sentence.

"The state will carry out the execution at midnight."

Idiom

CAUGHT RED-HANDED

Discovered in the middle of committing a crime.

"The thief was caught red-handed with the money."

Saying

AN EYE FOR AN EYE

The idea that punishment should exactly equal the crime.

"Their family strictly believes in an eye for an eye."

Noun/Slang

DEATH ROW

The specific prison area for inmates awaiting execution.

"He has been sitting on death row for fifteen years."

THE EXECUTION DEBATE

DAWN: I just don't understand how you can defend keeping him alive. He was caught red-handed—there's no doubt about his guilt.

GREG: Assuming that he is 100% guilty, the state still shouldn't have the power to kill its citizens. It's hypocritical.

DAWN: It's not hypocrisy; it's justice. If someone commits an atrocious crime, they shouldn't just be locked up with free meals—they need to face the ultimate consequence.

GREG: But executing him doesn't bring the victims back. It's just an eye for an eye. It doesn't solve the real issue.
