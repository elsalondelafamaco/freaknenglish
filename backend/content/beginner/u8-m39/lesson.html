Are your stories flat and boring?

Great gossip doesn't just use one time period. To tell a real story, you need to set the scene, drop the main action, and reveal the hidden secrets. Let's build some drama.

The Vibe

The Story Recipe

Don't worry about complicated grammar names. Just think about the THREE layers of a good story.

1. The Scene

What was in progress before the drama started?

"I was walking..."

2. The Action

The sudden event that changes everything.

"Suddenly, he shouted!"

3. The Secret

The reason why. Something that happened way before.

"Because she had lied."

Read this: "I heard a loud crash." Which layer of the story is this?

The Story

Don Arturo's Secret

Titó comes home with some explosive news about the neighbors. Pay attention to how he layers the drama.

Information Gap Challenge:

Why was Don Arturo so incredibly angry?

Titó: (whispering) "You won't believe this... While I was walking past Don Arturo's house, I heard him shout at his wife!"

El pa: (laughing) "No way! He never gets angry."

Titó: "Yes! While she was crying, he grabbed his coat and left the house. He was furious because he discovered that she had sold his favorite motorcycle!"

Gelmy: (shouting) "TITÓ! We do NOT spy on the neighbors! ... Wait, she sold his moto? Who did she sell it to?"
