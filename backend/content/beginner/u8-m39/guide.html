The Story Nuggets

📖
The Scene (Was/Were + ING)

The background vibe. It paints the picture of what was happening *before* the drama started.

Example: "The sun was shining and I was walking to work..."

The Action (Simple Past)

The quick, sudden things that actually push your story forward. The real drama!

Example: "...when a crazy dog jumped out and stole my shoe!"

When vs. While

**When** interrupts the scene completely. **While** connects two background scenes happening at the exact same time.

Example: "While I was running, Titó was laughing."

Suddenly

The magic transition word. Use this to shift from a boring "Scene" to an exciting "Action".

Example: "I was sleeping. Suddenly, I heard a scream!"

Flashback Nuggets

⏪
The Secret Past (Had + V3)

The "Past Perfect". Use this to drop a flashback. It's for an event that happened *before* your main story even started.

Example: "When I arrived, the party had already finished."

The "Because" Trick

We use the Secret Past to explain *why* something happened in the regular past.

Example: "Titó was crying because he had lost his keys."

Never / Already

These signal words love the Secret Past. They slide right into the middle, immediately after 'had'.

Example: "I had never seen snow before that trip."

By the time...

The ultimate connection phrase. Formula: "By the time [Action 2 happened], [Action 1 HAD already finished]."

Example: "By the time the police arrived, the thief had escaped."

🚀

The Micro-Mission

Record a voice note about a crazy disaster! Say:

"I was [Scene + ing] when suddenly [Action]! I was so mad because someone had [Secret Past + V3]!"
