The Vibe

Adjectives vs. Pronouns

ADJECTIVE (Needs the object):

"This is my car." / "That is your problem."

PRONOUN (Replaces the object):

"This car is mine." / "That problem is yours."

The Cheat Sheet

I ➔ Mine

You ➔ Yours

He ➔ His

She ➔ Hers

We ➔ Ours

They ➔ Theirs

Rule: Pronouns usually go at the END of the sentence!

The Story

The Living Room Interrogation

Gelmy is cleaning the house. She walks into the living room and stops. On the couch, there is an ugly jacket, some car keys, and a half-eaten empanada.

"Whose ugly jacket is this?!"

El pa panics: "It's not mine! Ask Titó, the empanada is his!"

Titó walks in, points at El pa, and says: "The keys are mine, but that ugly jacket is yours, El pa!"
