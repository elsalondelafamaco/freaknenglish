Cooking Nuggets

🔪

Chop vs. Slice

**Chop** means cutting into small messy pieces (Picar). **Slice** means cutting into clean, thin flat pieces (Rebanar).

"**Slice** the bread and **chop** the onions."

Boil vs. Fry

**Boil** uses bubbling hot water (Hervir). **Fry** uses hot oil or butter in a pan (Freír).

"**Boil** the pasta for 10 minutes, then **fry** the chicken."

Bake vs. Roast

Both use the oven! We **Bake** breads, cakes, and cookies. We **Roast** meats and vegetables.

"I'm going to **roast** beef and **bake** a pie."

Peel & Pour

**Peel** means taking the skin off fruit/veggies (Pelar). **Pour** means moving liquids into a container (Verter/Servir).

"**Peel** the potatoes and **pour** the milk."

!

The Recipe Flow

Sequence Words
  • 1. First, wash the vegetables.
  • 2. Next / Then, chop the tomatoes.
  • 3. After that, fry them in oil.
  • 4. Finally, serve and enjoy!

Paisa Secret:

Do not say "Cook the water" (Cocinar el agua). That sounds crazy! In English, if it's bubbling liquid, you must say "**Boil** the water"!

Arsenal Nuggets

🍳

The Pan vs. The Pot

A **Pan** is flat and used for frying eggs or meat (Sartén). A **Pot** is deep and used for boiling soup or pasta (Olla).

"Put the **pan** on the stove to fry the eggs."

The Stove vs. The Oven

The **Stove** is the top part with the burners (Estufa). The **Oven** is the box underneath where you bake things (Horno).

"Boil water on the **stove**, bake cookies in the **oven**."

Silverware (Cutlery)

The weapons of eating! A **Fork** (Tenedor) to stab, a **Knife** (Cuchillo) to cut, and a **Spoon** (Cuchara) for soup.

"Can I get a **fork** and **knife**, please?"

Measurements

When adding ingredients: A **Pinch** (una pizca), a **Handful** (un puñado), and a **Tablespoon** (cucharada).

"Add a **pinch** of salt and a **handful** of cheese."

!

The Menu

Flavor Profiles
  • Sweet (Dulce)
  • Salty (Salado)
  • Sour (Ácido / Limón)
  • Bitter (Amargo / Café)
  • Spicy (Picante)
The "Taste" Rule
  • "It tastes salty!"
  • "This soup tastes sour."

Street Tip:

If the food is amazing, don't just say "It's good." Say **"It's delicious!"** or **"It's mouth-watering!"** If it's terrible? Say **"It's disgusting!"**

The Masterchef Mission

Record a voice note about your favorite quick meal! Say: "**First**, I chop the [Food]. **Then**, I fry it in a [Pan/Pot]. It tastes very [Flavor]!"
