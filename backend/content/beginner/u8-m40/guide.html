Emergency Nuggets

🚨

The Stall (Buying Time)

Don't just stand there in silence! Say this to give your brain 3 seconds to translate.

"That's a good question. **Let me think...**"

The Rewind

Never just say "What?". It sounds rude. Use this to politely ask them to say it again.

"I'm sorry, **could you repeat that?**"

The Clarifier

When you hear a word you don't understand, don't pretend you do. Ask them to explain!

"Sorry, **what do you mean by** [word]?"

The Dictionary Cheat

If you know the word in Spanish but forgot the English version, ask them directly.

"**How do you say** 'multa' **in English?**"

!

The Lifesavers

Speed Control
  • Could you speak a little slower, please?
  • Sorry, I didn't catch that.
Memory Loss
  • I lost my train of thought.
  • It's on the tip of my tongue!

Paisa Secret:

Natives use filler words all the time. Saying "**Well...**" or "**Actually...**" before you answer makes you sound 10x more fluent while your brain searches for the vocabulary!

Opinion Nuggets

🧠

In my opinion...

Upgrade from "I think". Use this when discussing serious dilemmas or giving strong advice.

"**In my opinion**, we should call the police."

To be honest...

The perfect way to introduce a hard truth. It shows you are being direct but respectful.

"**To be honest**, I think that's a bad idea."

If I were you...

The ultimate problem-solving phrase. Put yourself in their shoes to give advice.

"**If I were you**, I would just apologize."

At the end of the day...

The best way to summarize your whole argument. It means "Al fin y al cabo".

"**At the end of the day**, health is more important."

!

The Debate

Structuring Thoughts
  • First of all, we need money.
  • Secondly, we don't have time.
  • On the other hand, it could be fun.
Finding a Fix
  • Why don't we...? (¿Por qué no...?)
  • How about we...? (¿Qué tal si...?)

Street Tip:

When native speakers are arguing a point, they love using the word **Basically**. "Basically, we need a new plan." It makes you sound confident and decisive.

The Problem Solver Mission

Record a voice note solving a dilemma! Say: "To be honest, that is a tough situation. But if I were you, I would [Action]. Basically, you need to [Action]!"
