THE TIPPING POINT

READING & CHECK

"I’m so sick of modern tipping culture. It feels like every time I buy something, a digital screen is shoved in my face asking for 15, 20, or even 25%. I went to a self-serve frozen yogurt place yesterday. I literally pulled the lever myself; nevertheless, it still asked for a tip! Who am I tipping? The iPad?

I completely understand tipping waiters because they scrape by on terrible base pay; however, this system is getting out of hand. Employers need to simply pay a living wage.

Why is it my responsibility to make sure their staff can make ends meet? The service industry is totally ripping us off. Am I the bad guy here?"

DETAIL

/ Who does the writer feel they are actually tipping at the yogurt shop?

/ Why does the writer believe waiters deserve tips, despite hating the system?

THINKING

/ Do you agree that digital tipping screens are getting out of hand?

/ Is it fair to expect customers to supplement an employee's salary?

VOCABULARY UPGRADE

Phrasal Verb

COUGH UP

To give money reluctantly.

"I didn't want to, but I had to cough up $50 for parking."

Phrasal Verb

RIP OFF

To deliberately overcharge someone.

"That mechanic completely ripped me off."

Phrasal Verb

SCRAPE BY

To manage to live on very little money.

"When I was in college, I was just scraping by."

Idiom

MAKE ENDS MEET

To have just enough money for basic living expenses.

"With rent so high, it's hard to make ends meet."

Slang

BROKE

Having absolutely zero money.

"I can't go out tonight; I'm completely broke."

Slang

BALLER

Someone who lives a very lavish, wealthy lifestyle.

"He's living like a baller since he got that promotion."

1. Have you ever had to cough up money unfairly?

2. Tell me about a time you felt totally ripped off.

3. Is it possible to live like a baller while scraping by?

LISTENING & ANALYSIS

DETAIL
  • / Why does the customer refuse to leave a tip?
  • / What does the server reveal about their actual base pay?
ANALYSIS
  • / Is the customer's argument logically sound, even if it's harsh?
  • / Who actually holds more power in this scenario: the customer or the server?
REACTION
  • / How would you handle this exact situation if you were the server?
  • / Would you ever confront a server like this customer did?

SERVER: Hi, here’s your check.

CUSTOMER: Thanks. Look, I’m not going to leave a tip. It’s nothing personal; whereas, I just don’t believe in tipping culture. Your employer should be paying you a fair wage.

SERVER: Excuse me? I rely on tips to make ends meet. My base pay is actually less than minimum wage because tips are expected by law.

CUSTOMER: I understand that; however, I think it’s a terrible system. It’s basically passing the cost of labor onto the consumer. If everyone stopped tipping, restaurants would be forced to pay a living wage.

SERVER: That’s a nice theory, but in reality, you’re just penalizing me for a system I didn’t create. If you can’t afford to tip, you shouldn’t eat out.

DEEP DISCUSSION

Do you feel a sense of guilt when you tap "No Tip" on a digital screen while the cashier watches?

Is it ethical to eat at a restaurant knowing the servers completely rely on your tips to survive?

Should the government intervene to legally abolish tipping and mandate higher minimum wages?

If a server provides aggressively terrible service, is leaving no tip finally justified, or just cruel?
